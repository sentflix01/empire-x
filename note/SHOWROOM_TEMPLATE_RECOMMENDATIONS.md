## 🔴 Category 1 — Business & Commerce (Additional)

> These fill major gaps in the current "Business & Commerce" category.

### 1.1 Fashion & Clothing Store
**Who:** Boutiques, second-hand shops, tailors, clothing importers
**Key Features:**
- Lookbook / seasonal collection gallery
- Size guide and color options table
- New arrivals + sale section
- WhatsApp order button
- Store locations / delivery zones

**Why:** Fashion is one of the top selling categories on Ethiopian social media. Boutique owners actively look for a way to show their catalogue online.

---

### 1.2 Electronics & Appliances Shop
**Who:** Mobile phone sellers, computer shops, appliance stores
**Key Features:**
- Product catalogue with specs (RAM, storage, brand)
- Price comparison table
- Warranty / after-sales section
- Import authenticity badge
- Repair services section

**Why:** Electronics is a high-demand category in Addis Ababa's Merkato and Bole areas. Sellers need a credible online presence.

---

### 1.3 Pharmacy & Drugstore
**Who:** Private pharmacies, drug wholesalers, health product stores
**Key Features:**
- Medicine categories (OTC, prescription, vitamins)
- Delivery service info
- Operating hours + emergency contact
- Pharmacist profile / credentials
- Insurance accepted list

**Why:** Ethiopia has thousands of private pharmacies. Having an online presence builds trust with customers.

---

### 1.4 Supermarket & Grocery Store

- Weekly deals / promotions banner

- Loyalty card / discount program

**Why:** Online grocery ordering is growing rapidly in Addis Ababa and other major cities.
