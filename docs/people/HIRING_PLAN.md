## Hiring Trigger Framework

Hire when a specific business condition is met — not by calendar:

| Role | Hire When | Not Before |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Full-Stack Engineer | Before public launch | — |
| Support Agent (1st) | >50 support tickets/day | Product-market fit signals |
| Support Agent (2nd) | Response time exceeds 8 hours | — |
| Growth Lead | D30 retention >35% + PMF confirmed | Premature growth kills companies |
| CTO / Lead Engineer | Post-seed funding closed | You need money to pay market rate |
| Product Manager | >3 active product streams simultaneously | Founder should own product longer than feels comfortable |
| Head of Partnerships | Series A closed | Partnership deals need full-time focus |
| General Counsel | Before first enterprise contract | Legal review can't be ad hoc at scale |

## Interview Process

### Standard Process (all roles)
```
Stage 1: Application review (resume + portfolio/GitHub)
  → Rejection: 48 hours max response

Stage 2: 30-min culture fit call (founder)
  → Questions: Why Nodbusiness? What matters to you? Tell me about X on your resume.
  → Goal: Assess communication, curiosity, alignment with mission

Stage 3: Technical/skills assessment (role-specific)
  → Engineers: 2-hour take-home task on a real Nodbusiness problem
  → Designers: Portfolio review + 30-min walkthrough
  → Support: Written test — respond to 5 simulated support tickets

Stage 4: Deep-dive interview (1 hour, cross-functional panel)
  → Structured questions + work through a real scenario together
  → Panel: Founder + one other relevant person

Stage 5: Reference checks (2 references, former managers preferred)
  → Ask: "On a scale of 1-10, how strongly would you rehire them? What would make it a 10?"

Stage 6: Offer → Sign NDA + employment contract → Start
```

### Time-to-hire target
```
Contractor roles: 2 weeks
Permanent roles: 3–4 weeks
Senior/C-suite: 6–8 weeks
```
