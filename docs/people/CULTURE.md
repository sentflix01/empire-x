> when nobody is watching and when it is hard to do the right thing.

## 1. Core Values

### Build Things That Matter
We do not build features for the sake of features. Every line of code,
every design decision, every business choice asks: does this create
real value for a real person running a real business?

### Default to Transparency
We share information openly — wins, losses, mistakes, strategy.
A team that knows what is happening can contribute to fixing it.
Hiding problems makes them worse.

### Respect People's Time
Fast decisions. Short meetings. Asynchronous by default.
We respect that everyone's time is finite and precious.

### Own the Outcome
Not just the task. Not just the ticket. The outcome.
If something is going wrong in your area, you do not wait to be asked.
You raise it, you propose a fix, you act.

### Global Mindset, Local Heart
We serve the world but we are deeply connected to Africa.
We understand local realities — infrastructure, languages, cultures —
and we build with them in mind, not as an afterthought.

### Build Trust, Not Just Features
Trust is earned slowly and lost instantly. In our products, in our team,
with our users, with investors. Everything we do is a trust decision.

## 2. How We Work

### Remote-First, Async-Preferred
```
Default: Write it down in Notion/docs before scheduling a meeting
Meeting rule: No meeting unless it can't be resolved async
Stand-ups: Async (written) daily, sync optional weekly
Core hours: 10:00–16:00 EAT (East Africa Time) — everyone available
```

### Decision-Making
```
Small decisions (affects 1–2 people): Make it yourself, document it
Medium decisions (affects a team or feature): Write a 1-page proposal, get feedback async, decide
Large decisions (affects the whole platform or company): Memo + discussion + founder makes the call
Reversible decisions: Move fast, try it, learn
Irreversible decisions: Slow down, think twice, get input
```

### Communication
```
Slack/Telegram: Quick questions, daily check-ins, informal
GitHub/Linear: All technical work, issues, PRs
Notion/Docs: All written decisions, knowledge, plans
Email: External only (customers, partners, investors)
Video call: Weekly team sync, onboarding, 1:1s
```

### Feedback Culture
```
Give feedback directly and kindly — not behind people's backs
"I noticed X. I think Y would work better because Z."
Receive feedback as a gift — it means someone cares enough to help you improve
Annual 360 reviews for everyone, including the founder
```

## 3. What We Celebrate

```
✅ Shipping something — anything — to users
✅ Catching a bug before a user does
✅ A user saying "this changed my business"
✅ An experiment that fails fast and teaches us something
✅ Someone asking for help rather than struggling alone
✅ Saying "I was wrong" — it is a sign of strength, not weakness
```

## 4. What We Do Not Tolerate

```
❌ Dishonesty — including by omission
❌ Blaming users for problems our product created
❌ Dismissing someone's idea without engaging with it
❌ Working in silence on a problem that someone else could help with
❌ Discrimination of any kind — race, gender, origin, religion, disability
❌ Taking credit for someone else's work
❌ Treating support tickets as annoyances rather than signal
```

## 5. The Nodbusiness Way (In Practice)

### When you get a bug report from a user:
1. Thank them — they took time to help us
2. Reproduce it
3. Fix it or create a ticket immediately
4. Follow up with the user when it is fixed

### When you disagree with a decision:
1. State your disagreement clearly with your reasoning
2. Give the decision-maker the chance to respond
3. If overruled: commit to executing the decision fully
4. If proved right: update the decision, no "I told you so"

### When you make a mistake:
1. Own it immediately — do not wait to be caught
2. Explain what happened and why
3. Fix it or get help fixing it
4. Document what you learned

### When you do not know something:
1. Say "I do not know" — not knowing is fine; pretending to know is not
2. Find out, or find who does know
3. Share what you learn so others benefit

## 6. Team Rituals

```
Daily:     Async standup (written — what did I do, what am I doing, any blockers?)
Weekly:    Team sync call (30 minutes — wins, blockers, demos)
Monthly:   All-hands (CEO update on metrics, strategy, priorities)
Quarterly: Retrospective (what went well, what to improve, what to stop)
Annual:    In-person team offsite (planning, bonding, culture)
```
