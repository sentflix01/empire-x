## 1. Our Remote Philosophy

Nodbusiness is remote-first by design, not by default.

**Remote-first** means:
- Processes are designed for remote from day one — not retrofitted
- All important decisions are written down (async-first communication)
- In-person time is intentional and high-value, not assumed
- Timezone diversity is a strength, not a scheduling problem

We operate as a global platform for global users. Our team should reflect and understand the world we serve.

## 2. Where Team Members Can Work

### Eligible Work Locations
Team members can work from:
- Their home country (primary location)
- Any country on our approved list for up to 90 days/year (without tax implications)
- Co-working spaces (approved for reimbursement up to $200/month)

### Legal Constraints
Working from a country creates potential tax and legal obligations:

| Duration | Status | Requirements |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| < 30 days in a country | Temporary | No action required; inform HR |
| 30–90 days | Extended stay | Check Permanent Establishment risk with Finance |
| > 90 days | Residency concern | Must be discussed with CEO and legal counsel |
| Permanent relocation | New home base | Contract update, EOR arrangement, or local entity required |

**Rule:** Always inform HR before working from a new country for more than 2 weeks.

## 3. Working Hours and Availability

### Core Overlap Hours
Nodbusiness requires all team members to be available for synchronous collaboration during:

**Core hours: 10:00–14:00 UTC, Monday–Friday**

This window works for:
- UTC-7 (West Coast US): 3–7 AM (stretch — not required to be available)
- UTC+0 (UK/West Africa): 10–14:00
- UTC+3 (East Africa / Middle East): 13–17:00
- UTC+5:30 (India): 15:30–19:30
- UTC+8 (Southeast Asia): 18–22:00

Team members choose their own schedule outside core hours.

### Expected Availability
```
Engineers:     Core hours + flexible (own schedule for deep work)
Support:       Rotating schedule covering time zones (separate rota)
CEO/Management: Core hours + async response within 4 hours generally
```

### Response Time Expectations
| Channel | Expected Response |
|---------|-----------------|
| Direct message (urgent) | Within 1 hour during work hours |
| Direct message (normal) | Within 4 hours |
| Email | Within 24 hours |
| GitHub PR review requests | Within 48 hours |
| RFC documents | Within 5 business days |

## 4. Communication Tools & Norms

### Tool Stack
| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Slack / Discord | Day-to-day async communication |
| Google Meet / Zoom | Video calls and team meetings |
| GitHub | Code, PRs, technical discussions |
| Linear / Jira | Task management, sprint tracking |
| Notion / Docs | Long-form documentation |
| Loom | Async video messages (instead of a meeting) |
| Email | External communication, formal communication |

### Communication Rules

**Default to async.** If a meeting can be a Loom video or a written document, it should be.

**Write things down.** Verbal decisions don't exist. If it matters, document it in Notion or Slack and link to it.

**Be explicit in text.** Tone is lost in text. Add context: "I'm not sure about this — can we discuss?" is better than "???"

**Meeting rules:**
- Every meeting has an agenda posted 24 hours before
- Every meeting ends with written action items, owners, and due dates
- Recurring meetings are reviewed quarterly — default is to cancel if no clear value
- No meeting longer than 60 minutes without a break

**No-meeting blocks:**
- Every team member is entitled to at least 2 consecutive no-meeting hours per day for deep work
- Engineers are encouraged to block mornings for deep work

## 5. Team Rituals

### Weekly
- **Monday async kickoff** — Each person posts in Slack: "This week I'm working on: [X]. My blockers are: [Y]."
- **Friday async recap** — Each person posts: "This week I completed: [X]. Next week: [Y]."

### Monthly
- **All-hands call (video, 60 minutes)** — Company updates, KPI review, shoutouts, Q&A
- **1:1 meetings** — Manager with each direct report: 30 minutes, recurring

### Quarterly
- **OKR review** — Team-wide review of objectives, results, and next quarter planning
- **Retrospective** — What went well? What did not? What do we change?

### Annually
- **Team offsites** — IRL (In Real Life) gatherings once or twice per year for bonding and strategic planning (once budget allows)

## 7. Security Requirements for Remote Work

All team members must:

```
□ Use a Nodbusiness-provided or approved device for work (no personal devices for code access)
□ Enable full disk encryption on their work machine
□ Use MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication) on all company accounts
□ Connect via VPN when accessing internal systems from public networks
□ Not use public Wi-Fi without a VPN
□ Lock their computer when stepping away (auto-lock after 5 minutes)
□ Never share passwords — use a password manager (1Password or Bitwarden)
□ Report lost or stolen devices within 1 hour to IT/CEO
□ Sign an NDA and IP assignment agreement before accessing any codebase
```

## 9. Mental Health and Disconnection

### Right to Disconnect
Team members are not expected to respond to messages outside their declared working hours except in genuine emergencies (P0 incidents). Managers must not pressure team members to respond outside hours.

### Paid Time Off (PTO)
- 20 days per year (accrued or upfront depending on contract)
- Unlimited PTO policy under consideration for Phase 2 (with tracking to ensure it is actually used)
- Minimum: Team members encouraged to take at least 10 days per year

### Sick Leave
- Unlimited sick days — team members should not work when ill
- No questions asked for sick days up to 5 consecutive days
- Doctor's note requested for 5+ consecutive sick days

### Mental Health
- Access to mental health support via company wellness program (Phase 2+)
- "No meeting Fridays" encouraged when possible
- Managers trained to check in, not just check up

## 10. Performance in a Remote Context

### How We Measure Performance
We measure **outcomes, not presence**:
- Did you ship what you committed to?
- Did you communicate proactively when blocked?
- Did your work meet quality standards?
- Did you support your teammates?

We do NOT use surveillance software, screenshot tools, or time-tracking that is invasive. We trust people we hire, and we hire people we can trust.

### Performance Issues
If a team member is underperforming remotely:
1. First: Check if it is a process/tooling/clarity problem (most common)
2. Second: 1:1 conversation with clear, specific feedback
3. Third: 30-day written improvement plan with specific outcomes
4. Fourth: Employment decision
