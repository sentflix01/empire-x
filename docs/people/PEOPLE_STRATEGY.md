## 3. Hiring Strategy

### 3.1 Hiring Principles

| Principle | Practice |
|-----------|----------|
| **Mission First** | Every hire must believe in African SMB empowerment |
| **Bar Raisers** | Every hire must raise the bar for their role |
| **Diversity by Design** | Structured interviews, diverse panels, blind resume review |
| **Local First** | Hire in Ethiopia, Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, SA — remote OK for senior |
| **Potential > Pedigree** | Skills, grit, learning velocity > brand names |
| **Referral First** | Employee referrals = highest quality, fastest close |

### 3.2 Hiring Process

| Stage | Duration | Owner | Pass Rate Target |
|-------|----------|-------|------------------|
| **1. Application Screen** | 2 days | Recruiter | 30% |
| **2. Phone Screen** | 30 min | Hiring Manager | 50% |
| **2. Technical Assessment** | 60-90 min | Senior IC | 40% |
| **3. System Design / Case Study** | 60 min | Senior IC + Peer | 50% |
| **4. Culture / Values** | 45 min | Cross-functional | 70% |
| **5. Reference Checks** | 2 days | Hiring Manager | 90% |
| **6. Offer** | 3 days | Recruiter + Founder | 80% |

**Total Target:** 3-4 weeks from screen to offer

### 3.3 Interview Rubrics

| Competency | Weight | Assessment |
|------------|--------|------------|
| **Technical Excellence** | 35% | Live coding, system design, code review |
| **Problem Solving** | 20% | Open-ended, ambiguous problems |
| **Communication** | 15% | Clarity, listening, async writing |
| **Mission Alignment** | 15% | Why Nodbusiness? Why Africa? Why now? |
| **Growth Mindset** | 10% | Learning velocity, feedback reception |
| **Collaboration** | 5% | Peer feedback, cross-functional examples |

## 4. Compensation Philosophy

### 4.1 Principles

| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Market Competitive** | 50-75th percentile for role/level/location |
| **Equity Heavy** | Early team: 60/40 cash/equity; Later: 70/30 |
| **Transparency** | Bands published internally; no negotiation theater |
| **Equity Refresh** | Annual refresh for top performers (10-20% of initial) |
| **No Negotiation** | Offer = best offer; bands are the bands |

### 4.2 Compensation Bands (Addis Ababa Base, USD)

| Level | Title | Base Salary | Equity (Initial) | Total Target |
|-------|-------|-------------|------------------|--------------|
| **L1** | Junior IC | $18K-$24K | 0.15-0.25% | $22K-$28K |
| **L2** | IC | $24K-$36K | 0.25-0.40% | $30K-$42K |
| **L3** | Senior IC | $36K-$54K | 0.40-0.75% | $45K-$70K |
| **L4** | Staff IC | $54K-$72K | 0.75-1.25% | $65K-$95K |
| **L5** | Principal IC | $72K-$96K | 1.25-2.0% | $90K-$130K |
| **L6** | Engineering Manager | $60K-$84K | 1.0-1.5% | $80K-$120K |
| **L7** | Director/VP | $84K-$132K | 2.0-4.0% | $130K-$200K |
| **C-Level** | CTO/VP/Head | $120K-$180K | 5-10% | $200K-$300K |

**Location Multipliers:** Nairobi 1.1x, Lagos 1.15x, Remote (Global) 1.2x, Remote (US/EU) 1.5x

## 5. Equity Plan

### 5.1 ESOP Pool

| Phase | Pool Size | Allocation |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| **Pre-Seed** | 10% | Founders 80%, ESOP 10%, Advisors 2% |
| **Seed** | 10% | Founders 68%, ESOP 10%, Seed 12%, Advisors 2% |
| **Series A** | 15% | Founders 51%, ESOP 15%, Series A 20%, Seed 9% |
| **Series B** | 15% | Founders 38%, ESOP 15%, Series B 18%, Series A 15% |

### 5.2 Grant Guidelines

| Role/Event | Grant Size (fully diluted) | Vesting |
|------------|---------------------------|---------|
| **Founding Team** | 15-25% each | 4yr, 1yr cliff |
| **First 10 Employees** | 0.5-2.0% | 4yr, 1yr cliff |
| **Senior Hires (L4+)** | 0.5-1.5% | 4yr, 1yr cliff |
| **Standard Hires (L1-L3)** | 0.1-0.5% | 4yr, 1yr cliff |
| **Annual Refresh (Top 20%)** | 10-20% of initial | 4yr, no cliff |
| **Promotion** | 25-50% of level delta | 4yr, no cliff |
| **Advisors** | 0.1-0.25% | 2yr, monthly vest |

### 5.3 Equity Mechanics

| Provision | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | ISO (Ethiopia/US employees), RSU (US/EU), Phantom (other) |
| **Exercise Window** | 90 days post-termination (extendable to 10yr for 5+ yr tenure) |
| **Early Exercise** | Allowed (83b election supported) |
| **Transfer Restrictions** | Right of first refusal, lockup per financing |
| **Liquidity** | Tender offers annually post-Series A; IPO/acquisition |

## 7. Compliance & Legal

### 7.1 Employment Law (Ethiopia Base)

| Requirement | Compliance |
|-------------|------------|
| **Labor Proclamation 1156/2019** | Contracts, hours, leave, termination |
| **Social Security** | 11% employer, 7% employee |
| **Income Tax** | Progressive (0-35%), withheld monthly |
| **Work Permits** | For foreign nationals (EOR where needed) |
| **Labor Union** | Not mandatory <10 employees |

### 7.2 Remote/International

| Country | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Kenya** | Local entity or EOR (Workpay, Deel) |
| **Nigeria** | Local entity or EOR |
| **Ghana** | EOR |
| **South Africa** | Local entity |
| **US/EU** | Direct (if employee) or Contractor |
| **Other** | Contractor (Deel/Remote.com) |

### 7.3 IP & Confidentiality

- **Invention Assignment** in all offer letters
- **NDA** signed pre-access to code/data
- **Non-compete** 12 months (Ethiopia enforceable, limited elsewhere)
- **Non-solicit** 12 months (employees, customers)
