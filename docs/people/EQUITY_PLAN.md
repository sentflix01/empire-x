## 4. Grant Guidelines

### 3.1 Initial Grants (Fully Diluted %)

| Role/Event | Grant Range | Vesting | Cliff |
|------------|-------------|---------|-------|
| **Founder/CEO** | 15-25% | 4 years | 1 year |
| **Co-Founders** | 10-20% | 4 years | 1 year |
| **First 10 Employees** | 0.5-2.0% | 4 years | 1 year |
| **Senior Hires (L4+)** | 0.5-1.5% | 4 years | 1 year |
| **Standard Hires (L1-L3)** | 0.1-0.5% | 4 years | 1 year |
| **Annual Refresh (Top 30%)** | 10-20% of initial | 4 years | None |
| **Promotion** | 25-50% of level delta | 4 years | None |
| **Advisors** | 0.1-0.25% | 2 years | Monthly |
| **Board Members** | 0.25-0.5% | 3 years | Monthly |

### 3.2 Grant Sizing Formula

```
Grant % = Base % × Location Multiplier × Role Multiplier × Seniority Multiplier
```

| Factor | Multiplier |
|--------|------------|
| **Location** | Addis=1.0x, Nairobi=1.0x, Lagos=1.0x, Remote=1.0x |
| **Role** | Eng=1.0x, Product=0.9x, Design=0.9x, GTM=0.8x, Ops=0.7x, G&A=0.6x |
| **Seniority** | L1=0.5x, L2=0.75x, L3=1.0x, L4=1.5x, L5=2.0x, L6=2.5x, L7=3.0x |

## 5. Vesting & Exercise

### 4.1 Standard Vesting

| Schedule | Detail |
|---------|---------|
| **Standard** | 4 years, monthly after 1-year cliff |
| **Advisors** | 2 years, monthly vest, no cliff |
| **Board Members** | 3 years, monthly vest, no cliff |
| **Refresh/Promotion** | 4 years, no cliff |

### 4.2 Acceleration Triggers

| Trigger | Single | Double |
|---------|--------|--------|
| **Change of Control** | ✅ | ✅ (Founders/Executives) |
| **Involuntary Termination (w/o Cause)** | ✅ (12 months) | ✅ (12 months) |
| **Termination for Cause** | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Voluntary Resignation** | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Death/Disability** | ✅ (Full) | ✅ (Full) |

**Single Trigger:** Vesting accelerates on trigger event
**Double Trigger:** Requires both CoC + termination within 12 months

### 4.3 Exercise Mechanics

| Provision | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Exercise Window** | 90 days post-termination (extendable to 10 years for 5+ yr tenure) |
| **Early Exercise** | Allowed (83(b) election supported) |
| **Cashless Exercise** | Available at liquidity events |
| **Cashless Sell-to-Cover** | Available at liquidity |
| **Transfer Restrictions** | Right of First Refusal (ROFR), Lock-up per financing |
| **Transfer to Family Trust** | Permitted (with Board consent) |

## 7. Tax Considerations

### 7.1 Ethiopia

| Aspect | Treatment |
|--------|-----------|

| Election | Deadline | Benefit |
|----------|----------|---------|
