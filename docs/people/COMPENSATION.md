> **How we pay people fairly and create shared ownership.**
> Compensation is one of the clearest signals of what a company values.

## 1. Compensation Philosophy

```
Principle 1: Pay market rate — we do not underpay and expect loyalty
Principle 2: Equity for everyone — every full-time employee gets meaningful equity
Principle 3: Transparent bands — no salary secrets within the team
Principle 4: Pay equity — same role, same experience = same pay, regardless of gender/origin
Principle 5: Review annually — compensation grows as the company grows
```

## 3. Equity Structure

### Option Pool (ESOP)
```
Phase 1: 10% option pool
Phase 2: Top up to 15% before seed round
Phase 3: Top up to 18% before Series A
```

### Vesting Schedule (Standard)
```
Duration: 4 years
Cliff: 1 year (25% vests after 12 months)
After cliff: Monthly vesting (1/36th per month for 36 months)
Total: 100% vested at 4 years
```

## 4. Option Exercise

```
Strike price: Fair Market Value (FMV) at time of grant
  → Early stage (pre-revenue): typically $0.001–$0.01/share
  → Post-seed: set by 409A valuation (US) or equivalent
  → Post-Series A: set by latest round price × discount

Exercise window:
  → While employed: anytime after vesting
  → After leaving: 90 days to exercise (standard)
  → Extended window (preferred): 5–10 years (founder choice — highly recommended)
  
Note: Extended exercise window (5–10 years) is one of the most employee-friendly
policies a company can offer. It prevents employees from losing options simply
because they can't afford to exercise within 90 days. LinkedIn, Pinterest,
and many top companies use extended windows.
```

## 6. Pay Equity Process

```
Annual process (every January):
  1. HR/Ops pulls all salaries anonymized by level
  2. Compare across gender, nationality, location (for same-location hires)
  3. Identify gaps > 5% for same role, same level, same experience
  4. Budget remediation raises before annual review cycle
  5. Publish aggregate pay equity statistics to the team (not individual salaries)
```
