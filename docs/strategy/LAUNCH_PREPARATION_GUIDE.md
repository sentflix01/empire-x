## Step 2 — Register Your Domain Names

### 📌 What it is
A domain name is your address on the internet (like nodbusiness.com).
Whoever registers a domain first owns it. Registration costs $10–15 per year.
Without registration, anyone can take your domain and sell it back to you
for thousands of dollars (called "domain squatting").

### ⚠️ Why you must do it TODAY
Domain names are registered on a first-come, first-served basis.
There is no legal protection until you register. A squatter can register
nodbusiness.com today and demand $5,000–50,000 to sell it to you.
For $10, you eliminate this risk permanently.

### 🎯 Exact Action

**Step 2.1 — Choose your registrar**
Use **Cloudflare Registrar** (https://cloudflare.com/registrar).

Why Cloudflare:
- Sells domains at cost (no markup — cheapest option)
- Free WHOIS privacy (hides your personal address from public)
- Excellent DNS management (you will need this for email and deployment)
- Used by millions of professional businesses

**Step 2.2 — Create a Cloudflare account**
1. Go to: https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up
2. Enter email: use your business email if possible
3. Verify email
4. You now have a Cloudflare account

**Step 2.3 — Register these domains in order of priority**

**Priority 1 — Register TODAY:**
```

## Step 4 — Timestamp Your Codebase (Proof of Creation)

### 📌 What it is
A blockchain timestamp is an irrefutable record of when a document existed.
It creates a cryptographic hash of your file and records it on the Bitcoin
blockchain — a permanent, unalterable public ledger. This proves you created
your software before any specific date, which matters in patent priority
disputes and copyright claims.

### ⚠️ Why you must do it TODAY
If a competitor later claims they built their platform before you and files
a patent, your blockchain timestamp proves your priority date. This costs
nothing and takes 5 minutes. Skipping it means you rely only on your Git
history (which is good evidence but not as strong as blockchain timestamps).

### 🎯 Exact Action

**Step 4.1 — Create a snapshot of your codebase**
1. Open your project folder: `C:\Users\Administrator\Desktop\app\pro-v1`
2. Create a ZIP file of the entire project (excluding node_modules and .next)
3. Name it: `nodbusiness-source-[today's date].zip`
   Example: `nodbusiness-source-2026-08-15.zip`

**Step 4.2 — Timestamp on OriginStamp (Free)**
1. Go to: **https://originstamp.com**
2. Click "Submit"
3. Upload your ZIP file
4. OriginStamp calculates the SHA-256 hash of your file
5. This hash is written to the Bitcoin blockchain within 24 hours
6. You receive a certificate PDF proving the file existed on this date
7. **Download and save this certificate — it is your legal evidence**

**Step 4.3 — Also timestamp these documents**
Repeat the process for:
- This `LAUNCH_PREPARATION_GUIDE.md`
- `GO_TO_MARKET.md`
- `docs/strategy/README.md`
- `docs/SOP.md`
- Any written business plan or concept document

**Step 4.4 — Email yourself a backup**
1. Attach the ZIP file and the certificate PDFs to an email
2. Send it to yourself AND to a trusted person (family member, lawyer)
3. Subject line: "Nodbusiness Codebase Timestamp - [Date]"
4. Do NOT delete this email — ever

**Step 4.5 — Your Git history is also evidence**
Your GitHub repository already contains:
- Every commit with timestamp
- Who made each commit
- What was changed in each commit

This is accepted as evidence in most courts. Print or export your commit
history periodically and keep a copy offline.

### 💰 Cost
Free (OriginStamp basic plan)

### ⏱️ Time
20 minutes

### ✅ Done when
You have a PDF certificate from OriginStamp showing today's date and the hash of your source code, saved in a secure location.

## Step 7 — Sign NDAs with Anyone Who Has Seen Your Code

### 📌 What it is
An NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) is a legal contract where another person
agrees to keep your confidential information secret. It is enforceable in court.

### ⚠️ Why this week?
Every day without an NDA is a risk. If a developer, advisor, or partner
who has seen your code launches a competing platform, you have limited
legal recourse without a signed NDA. This is especially important for the
AI pricing algorithm — your most valuable technical asset.

### 🎯 Exact Action

**Step 7.1 — Identify who needs to sign**
Make a list of:
- Any developer who has worked on the codebase
- Any business advisor who has seen your strategy
- Any potential investors who have seen your pitch
- Any contractors (designers, copywriters) who created assets for Nodbusiness

**Step 7.2 — Get an NDA template**
Your attorney will provide a customized one. In the meantime, use this structure:

```
NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
[DATE] between Nodbusiness Technologies ("Disclosing Party") and
[NAME] ("Receiving Party").

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
The Receiving Party agrees to keep confidential all information disclosed
by Nodbusiness Technologies, including but not limited to:
- Source code, algorithms, and technical architecture
- Business strategy, roadmap, and financial projections
- User data and database contents
- Marketing strategies and partner relationships
- The AI template pricing algorithm specifically

2. OBLIGATIONS
The Receiving Party agrees not to:
a) Disclose confidential information to any third party
b) Use confidential information to build a competing product
c) Copy, reproduce, or distribute the source code

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
All work created for Nodbusiness Technologies is the sole property of
Nodbusiness Technologies. The Receiving Party assigns all intellectual
property rights in such work to Nodbusiness Technologies.

4. TERM
This agreement is effective from the date signed and remains in effect
for 5 years after the relationship with Nodbusiness Technologies ends.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of Ethiopia.

Signed: _____________________ Date: _____________
[Receiving Party Name and Signature]

Signed: _____________________ Date: _____________
[Nodbusiness Technologies Representative]
```

**Step 7.3 — Get it signed**
- Send via email asking for signature
- For digital signature: use DocuSign (free tier) or Smallpdf eSign
- Keep a copy of every signed NDA in a secure folder
- File both physical and digital copies

**Step 7.4 — Future contractor onboarding process**
From now on, NO ONE touches the code without signing the NDA first.
This is non-negotiable. Make it part of your onboarding process:
1. Send NDA for e-signature
2. Wait for signed copy
3. Then share any code, strategy, or confidential information

### 💰 Cost
NDA template from attorney: $100–300 (one-time, use for all future signings)
DocuSign free tier: $0

### ⏱️ Time
2–4 hours to identify people + send documents
1–2 days to receive signatures

### ✅ Done when
Every person who has seen your code or strategy has signed a dated NDA and you have copies of all signed agreements in a secure location.

## Step 9 — Register Your Company

### 📌 What it is
A company registration creates a legal entity separate from you as a person.
Without it, you are personally liable for any debt, lawsuit, or legal issue
that arises from Nodbusiness. With a registered company, the company is liable —
not you personally.

### ⚠️ Why this month?
You need a registered company to:
- Open a business bank account (Chapa and Stripe require this)
- Sign legal contracts
- Accept investment from any investor
- File a trademark in the company name
- Hire employees legally
- Collect payments without personal tax issues

### 🎯 Exact Action

**Option A: Ethiopian Private Limited Company (PLC) — Register First**

A PLC (Private Limited Company) is the standard business structure in Ethiopia.

**Required documents:**
```
1. Memorandum of Association
   → Defines: company name, purpose, shareholders, share capital
   → Drafted by your attorney (~$200–500)

2. Articles of Association
   → Defines: internal rules, voting rights, director powers
   → Drafted by your attorney

3. Shareholder list
   → At minimum: you + one other person (can be a family member)
   → You can own 99% and they own 1%

4. Director information
   → Name, ID/passport, address of all directors
   → At minimum 1 director (you)

5. Registered office address
   → A physical address in Ethiopia where legal notices can be sent
   → Can be your home address initially
   → Or use your attorney's office address

6. Business purpose description
   → "Technology platform providing social networking, e-commerce,
      and business tools via software applications"
```

**Registration process:**
```
Step 1: Have attorney draft Memorandum + Articles of Association
Step 2: Get all documents notarized
Step 3: Open a temporary bank account (some banks require company docs first — ask your attorney for the chicken-and-egg solution they use in practice)
Step 4: Pay initial share capital to the bank (minimum varies — often 10,000–50,000 ETB)
Step 5: Submit all documents to:
        Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration (MoTRI)
        OR Ethiopian Investment Commission (EIC) if qualifying as a tech startup
Step 6: Receive Business Registration Certificate
Step 7: Register for Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) at Ethiopian Revenue Authority
Step 8: Open permanent business bank account with registration certificate
```

**Registration offices:**
- Ethiopian Investment Commission (EIC): investment.gov.et
- Ministry of Trade: trade.gov.et
- Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (for initial bank account)

**Step 9.1 — Company naming convention**
Register as: **Nodbusiness Technologies PLC** or **Nodbusiness PLC**

**Option B: Delaware C-Corporation (USA) — Register Before Fundraising**

Register this when you are ready to raise international investment.
Delaware is the standard for US-based and global startups because:
- Investors expect and understand it
- Clean equity structure for stock options
- Strong shareholder protections

**How to register Delaware C-Corp:**
```
Step 1: Go to: https://stripe.com/atlas OR https://clerky.com
        (Both handle the full Delaware C-Corp setup for ~$500)
        OR go directly to Delaware at: corp.delaware.gov ($109 filing fee)

Step 2: Choose company name: Nodbusiness Technologies Inc.

Step 3: Provide:
        - Registered agent in Delaware ($50–200/year)
        - Director name and address
        - Authorized shares (typically 10,000,000 shares)

Step 4: Receive Certificate of Incorporation (usually same day)

Step 5: Create bylaws and initial share structure
        (templates provided by Stripe Atlas or Clerky)

Step 6: Open a US bank account (Mercury, Relay, or Brex — no minimum balance)
```

**Recommended approach:**
Register Ethiopian PLC first (for local operations).
Register Delaware C-Corp before your first investor meeting (for international capital).

### 💰 Cost
Ethiopian PLC: ~$150–500 (government fees) + $300–800 (attorney)
Delaware C-Corp via Stripe Atlas: ~$500 (all-inclusive)
Annual Delaware registered agent: ~$50–200/year

### ⏱️ Time
Ethiopian PLC: 2–4 weeks
Delaware C-Corp: 1–5 business days

## Step 10 — File Your Trademark in Ethiopia

### 📌 What it is
A trademark registration gives you the exclusive legal right to use the name
"Nodbusiness" and your crown logo in commerce in Ethiopia, with priority rights
internationally (Paris Convention). It is the single most important legal filing
for protecting your brand.

### ⚠️ Why this month?
The moment you file your trademark, that date becomes your "priority date."
If anyone else tries to register "Nodbusiness" anywhere in the world within
6 months, YOUR date takes precedence — even if you haven't filed in their
country yet. This is the Paris Convention right. Every month you delay,
a competitor can establish a stronger priority date.

### 🎯 Exact Action

**Step 10.1 — Prepare your trademark application**

Your attorney will handle the filing, but you need to provide:

```
1. Applicant name and address
   → "Nodbusiness Technologies PLC" (your registered company)
   → Registered office address in Ethiopia

2. Trademark representation
   → Word mark: NODBUSINESS (all capitals, black on white)
   → Design mark: Your crown logo (PNG, 300dpi minimum, on white background)
   → File both: one application for the word, one for the logo

3. Classes to file in (Nice Classification):
   → Class 9:  Computer software, mobile applications
   → Class 35: Business management, advertising, marketplace services
   → Class 36: Financial transaction services, payment processing
   → Class 38: Telecommunications, online messaging services
   → Class 42: Software as a Service (SaaS), cloud computing

4. Description of goods/services (example for Class 9):
   "Computer software for social networking, e-commerce, business
   showroom creation, task management, messaging, and payment processing;
   mobile applications for the above purposes."
```

**Step 10.2 — File at EIPA**

Ethiopian Intellectual Property Authority (EIPA)
- Website: www.eipa.gov.et
- Address: Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
- Your attorney will visit in person with the documents
- Fee: approximately 100–300 ETB per class per mark

**Step 10.3 — After filing — CRITICAL**
Immediately after filing, note your filing date.
This date is your priority date for international filings.

You now have 6 months to file in other countries and claim this same
priority date (Paris Convention Article 4). After 6 months, this right expires.

**Step 10.4 — Plan your international filing sequence**
Within 6 months of your Ethiopian filing, file in:
1. USA (USPTO) — most important for global investors
2. EU (EUIPO) — covers all 27 European countries at once
3. OR file Madrid Protocol through WIPO (covers 130+ countries in one application)

Discuss this timeline and budget with your attorney before filing in Ethiopia.

### 💰 Cost
Government fees: ~$100–300 (for 2 marks × 5 classes)
Attorney fees: ~$300–800
**Total: ~$400–1,100**

### ⏱️ Time
Application preparation: 1–2 weeks (with attorney)
EIPA processing time: 6–12 months to receive the certificate
BUT: You are protected from the FILING DATE, not the certificate date.

### ✅ Done when
You have filed your trademark application at EIPA and received a filing receipt with a date stamp. This receipt is your proof of priority date. Keep it very safely.

has users from these regions and you do not provide these features,

You need this before your first real user signs up. Building it before

and regulators asking questions.

### 🎯 Exact Action

In the Account Settings page, add a button: "Download My Data"

- All orders (as buyer and seller)
- All messages (conversations and messages)
- Account activity log (login times)

This is already in the strategy at `docs/strategy/v2.1-accessibility-i18n/`.

   - All personal messages
   - Payment/wallet data (keep for legal record-keeping, anonymize user link)
4. Send confirmation email: "Your Nodbusiness account has been deleted."

## Step 13 — File Trademark in USA

### 📌 What it is
USPTO (United States Patent and Trademark Office) registration gives you
trademark rights in the USA — the world's largest tech market. It also
signals to global investors that you have protected your brand.

### ⚠️ Why before Phase 2?
When you start charging users, Nodbusiness becomes commercially valuable.
Competitors will notice and may try to register similar marks in the USA
before you. Filing before Phase 2 launch protects you.

Using your Ethiopian priority date (Paris Convention), you can file within
6 months of your Ethiopian filing and claim the same priority date.

### 🎯 Exact Action

**Option A: File yourself at USPTO.gov (cheapest)**
```
1. Go to: https://tmsearch.uspto.gov
2. Search "Nodbusiness" to confirm no conflicts
3. If clear, go to: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply
4. Use TEAS Plus form ($250 per class × 5 classes = $1,250 total)
5. Complete the application:
   → Applicant: Nodbusiness Technologies Inc. (Delaware Corp) or Nodbusiness Technologies PLC
   → Mark: NODBUSINESS (word mark) — do the logo separately
   → International Class: 9, 35, 36, 38, 42
   → Goods and services description (must be from USPTO's ID Manual)
   → Specimen: screenshot of the app showing the mark in use (or intent-to-use filing)
   → Filing basis: Section 44(d) based on Ethiopian application (uses your priority date)
6. Pay online
7. Receive filing receipt with serial number

Important: TEAS Plus requires you to specify services from the USPTO ID Manual.
Your attorney can help with the exact descriptions.
```

**Option B: Use a trademark attorney (recommended for first-time filers)**
```
Attorney handles everything. You provide:
→ Company information
→ Logo files
→ Description of services
→ Ethiopian application filing receipt (for priority date)

Attorney cost: $500–1,500 (in addition to USPTO fees)
Worth it for your first international filing.
```

**What happens after filing:**
```
1. USPTO examiner reviews application (3–4 months)
2. If approved: published in Official Gazette for 30 days (opposition period)
3. If no opposition: registration issues in ~10 months total
4. You receive US Trademark Registration Certificate
5. Trademark valid for 10 years, renewable
```

### 💰 Cost
USPTO fees: $250 × 5 classes = $1,250
Attorney: $500–1,500 (optional but recommended)
**Total: $1,250–2,750**

### ⏱️ Time
Filing: 1–2 weeks (with attorney)
Full registration: 8–12 months from filing date

### ✅ Done when
You receive a USPTO serial number and filing receipt, confirming your application is in the USPTO system.

## Step 14 — File Provisional Patent Applications

### 📌 What it is
A provisional patent application is a 12-month "placeholder" that:
1. Gives you "Patent Pending" status immediately
2. Establishes your priority date for the invention
3. Costs much less than a full patent
4. Gives you 12 months to decide if you want to file the full patent

It is NOT a granted patent. But "Patent Pending" means competitors cannot
copy your method and patent it themselves.

### ⚠️ Why before Phase 2?
The moment Nodbusiness launches publicly with your AI pricing algorithm,
competitors can see it, analyze it, and file their own patent before you.
Filing a provisional before launch protects you.

You have identified 3 patentable inventions:
1. AI Dynamic Template Pricing Method
2. Developer Revenue Share Quality-Adjustment System
3. Combined Social-Commerce Showroom Discovery Method

### 🎯 Exact Action

**Step 14.1 — Document each invention clearly**

For each of the 3 inventions, write a description in plain language:

```
Patent 1: AI Dynamic Template Pricing

Problem it solves:
Template prices on existing platforms are fixed (Shopify: $X/month for all).
This doesn't reflect real market demand or template quality.

How it works:
1. The system continuously monitors: how many times each template is viewed,
   how many businesses install it, how long they keep using it, and user ratings.
2. These signals are combined with a competition factor (how many similar
   businesses exist in the same category on the platform).
3. An algorithm calculates a "demand score" for each template.
4. The template price is adjusted automatically within a range set by the developer.
5. Users see a "High Demand" or "Popular" indicator if the price has risen.

What makes it novel:
No existing platform dynamically prices templates based on real-time demand,
quality scores, and competition density simultaneously.
```

Write a similar description for Patents 2 and 3.

**Step 14.2 — Find a patent attorney**
Ask your IP attorney for referrals to a patent attorney with:
- Technology/software patent experience
- USPTO registration (required for US patents)
- Experience with method patents (not just product patents)

**Step 14.3 — File the provisional application**
Your patent attorney will:
1. Review your invention descriptions
2. Draft the provisional patent specification
3. File with USPTO (if targeting USA) at uspto.gov
4. Pay the filing fee ($320 small entity / $160 micro entity)
5. You receive a provisional application number

**Note:** Provisional patent applications are ONLY valid for 12 months.
Within 12 months, you must file the full (utility) patent application
or the provisional expires.

**Step 14.4 — Keep the algorithm secret meanwhile**
Even with a patent application pending, protect the algorithm as a trade secret:
- Store the pricing algorithm code in a separate, access-controlled repository
- Only 1–2 engineers should have access
- Document access logs (who viewed the code and when)
- Never expose the algorithm logic to the frontend

### 💰 Cost
Attorney fees for 3 provisional applications: $3,000–6,000
USPTO fees: ~$480 (3 × $160 micro entity)
**Total: $3,500–6,500**

### ⏱️ Time
Drafting: 2–4 weeks
Filing: 1 day after drafting complete

### ✅ Done when
You have provisional patent application numbers from USPTO for all 3 inventions and you can legally say "Patent Pending" on your platform.

## Step 15 — Legal Review of All User-Facing Documents

### 📌 What it is
Before charging users money, have a licensed attorney review and sign off on:
- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Developer Agreement (for Phase 2 template marketplace)
- Seller Agreement (for marketplace transactions)

### ⚠️ Why before Phase 2?
A poorly written Terms of Service has caused billion-dollar companies
to be sued successfully. Your Terms of Service is a contract that:
- Limits your liability for user content
- Defines your right to terminate accounts
- Governs dispute resolution
- Defines intellectual property ownership

Without attorney review, any of these could be unenforceable or expose you
to unlimited liability.

### 🎯 Exact Action

Your existing legal docs in `docs/legal/`:
1. `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` — review for enforceable limitation of liability

3. `SELLER_AGREEMENT.md` — review for payment terms + dispute resolution
4. Create: `DEVELOPER_AGREEMENT.md` — for Phase 2 template marketplace

**Step 15.2 — Key clauses attorney must verify**
```
Terms of Service:
→ Limitation of liability clause (caps your liability)
→ Indemnification clause (users protect you from third-party claims)
→ Dispute resolution + arbitration clause (reduces litigation)
→ Class action waiver (prevents class-action lawsuits)

Developer Agreement (Phase 2):
→ Revenue split clearly defined (70% developer / 30% Nodbusiness)

3. Presented again during first payment (confirmation screen)
4. Versioned with dates (when last updated)
5. Email notification sent to all users when updated significantly

## Step 16 — Monitor and Enforce Your Trademark

### 📌 What it is
A trademark only has value if you enforce it. If you allow others to use
"Nodbusiness" without objection, you can lose trademark rights through "abandonment."

### 🎯 Exact Action

**Set up monitoring:**
```
1. Google Alerts for "Nodbusiness" (already done in Step 3)
2. Social media monitoring: check monthly for @nodbusiness impersonators
3. App store monitoring: search "Nodbusiness" in App Store and Play Store monthly
4. Domain monitoring: set alerts on Cloudflare for domains similar to yours
   → haIehame.com (capital i instead of l — looks identical)
   → hallehame.com (double l)
   → nodbusiness.net (different TLD)
```

**When you find a potential infringer:**
```
Step 1: Document everything (screenshot with URL and date)
Step 2: Check if it is a genuine trademark conflict or coincidence
Step 3: Send a cease-and-desist letter (your attorney drafts this)
Step 4: If no response: escalate to platform takedown (DMCA, App Store report)
Step 5: If continued: consult attorney for legal action
```

**Trademark renewal:**
```
Ethiopian trademark: renew every 7 years
US trademark: file maintenance documents at 5–6 years and 9–10 years, then renew every 10 years
Keep calendar reminders for all renewal dates
```

### 💰 Cost
Ongoing monitoring: Free (Google Alerts) or $50–200/year for professional monitoring service
Cease-and-desist letter: $300–800 (attorney)

### ⏱️ Time
Monthly review: 30 minutes
Response to infringement: 1–2 weeks
