## 1. GTM Philosophy

### The Core Insight: **Businesses Bring Their Customers**

Every business that publishes a showroom brings their existing customers (WhatsApp contacts, Instagram followers, walk-ins) onto the platform. **The business IS the acquisition channel.**

```
1 Business publishes showroom
        │
        ▼
  Shares link on WhatsApp/Instagram/Telegram
        │
        ▼
  50–500 existing customers click
        │
        ▼
  10–50 create accounts (customers)
        │
        ▼
  Customers follow, browse, buy
        │
        ▼
  Network effects compound
```

**This is not B2B SaaS. This is B2B2C network-led growth.**

## 8. Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| **Low Viral Coefficient** | Medium | High | Optimize share flow, improve incentives, test copy |
| **High Churn (Free → Paid)** | High | High | Usage-based triggers, value demonstration, onboarding |
| **Payment Failures (Local)** | High | High | Multiple gateways, COD fallback, wallet escrow |
| **Competitor Copy** | High | Medium | Patents (4 filed), speed, network effects, brand |
| **Regulatory (Data/Payments)** | Medium | High | Local counsel, compliance roadmap, SOC2 |
| **Talent Retention** | Medium | High | Equity, mission, culture, competitive comp |
