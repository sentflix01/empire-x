# Nodbusiness — Voice & Tone Guide

> **How Nodbusiness communicates with the world.**
> Voice is who we are. Tone is how we adapt to the situation.
> Every word written for or about Nodbusiness follows this guide.

## 1. Our Voice (Always the Same)

Nodbusiness always sounds:

### Direct
We say what we mean in plain language. No corporate speak, no jargon, no filler.
```
❌ "We leverage cutting-edge synergistic solutions to empower your digital journey"
✅ "Build your business website in 10 minutes"
```

### Warm but Not Childish
We care about the people we serve. But we are not a toy or a game.
```
❌ "Yay! Your showroom is LIVE! 🎉🎊🥳"
✅ "Your showroom is live. Here's the link to share with customers."
```

### Confident but Not Arrogant
We know what we're doing, but we don't talk down to users.
```
❌ "Obviously, you'll want to add your products first."
✅ "Add your products to get started."
```

### Global but Locally Aware
We serve the world, but we understand local contexts.
```
❌ "Pay with your credit card"
✅ "Pay with card, mobile money, or bank transfer"
```

## 2. Tone (Adapts to Situation)

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|
| Onboarding | Warm, encouraging | "Welcome! Let's set up your first showroom." |
| Success states | Celebratory but brief | "Order placed. You'll hear from the seller soon." |
| Error messages | Calm, helpful, not blaming | "Couldn't load the feed. Check your connection and try again." |
| Legal / compliance | Clear, formal, precise | "By using Nodbusiness, you agree to our Terms of Service." |
| Marketing | Energetic, aspirational | "Your business. The world's customers." |
| Support | Empathetic, solution-focused | "We understand this is frustrating. Here's what you can do." |
| Empty states | Encouraging, actionable | "No orders yet. Share your showroom to start selling." |

## 3. Writing Rules

### Use Active Voice
```
❌ "Your order has been placed by you"
✅ "You placed an order"

❌ "An error was encountered"
✅ "Something went wrong"
```

### Use Present Tense
```
❌ "Your showroom will be published"
✅ "Your showroom is now live"
```

### Be Specific
```
❌ "There was a problem"
✅ "Couldn't save your changes — check your internet connection"

❌ "An error occurred"
✅ "Payment failed. Your card was declined."
```

### Write for Everyone
```
❌ "Please ensure you have configured your SMTP relay correctly"
✅ "Check your email settings"

❌ "The API endpoint returned a 403 Forbidden response"
✅ "You don't have permission to do this"
```

### Numbers and Currency
```
✅ 4,200 users (not 4200)
✅ $29/month (not $29 per month or USD 29)
✅ ETB 1,500 (not 1500 ETB)
✅ 3× more (not 3x or 3 times more)
```

## 4. UI Copy Standards

### Buttons
```
✅ Verb + noun: "Save Changes", "List Product", "Send Message"
✅ Specific: "Publish Showroom" not "Submit"
✅ Short: Max 3 words
❌ Vague: "Okay", "Submit", "Yes"
❌ Long: "Click here to save your changes to the database"
```

### Error Messages
```
Structure: [What happened] + [Why it might have happened] + [What to do]
Example: "Couldn't save your profile. Check your internet and try again."

Never say:
  ❌ "Error 500: Internal Server Error"
  ❌ "Something went wrong. Please try again later." (too vague)
  ❌ "You did something wrong." (blaming user)
```

### Empty States
```
Structure: [What's empty] + [Why it's valuable] + [How to fill it]
Example:
  Title: "No products yet"
  Body:  "List your first product to start selling to local customers."
  CTA:   "List a Product"
```

### Success Messages
```
Short. Specific. Reassuring.
✅ "Message sent"
✅ "Showroom published — here's your link"
✅ "Payment received"
❌ "Your request has been successfully processed and a confirmation email has been sent to your registered email address."
```

### Loading States
```
✅ "Loading your feed..."
✅ "Saving..."
✅ "Processing payment..."
❌ Spinners with no label (always add descriptive text for screen readers)
```

## 5. Inclusive Language

```
✅ Use "they/them" when gender is unknown: "When a seller lists a product, they..."
✅ Use "business owner" not "businessman"
✅ Use "everyone" not "guys"
✅ Use "sign in" not "login" (verb form: "log in" — but "login" as a noun is fine)
✅ Avoid idioms that don't translate globally: "hit the ground running", "touch base"
✅ Use plain equivalents: "start immediately", "connect"
❌ Do not use: blacklist/whitelist (use: blocklist/allowlist)
❌ Do not use: master/slave (use: primary/replica)
❌ Do not use: dummy data (use: sample data, placeholder data)
```

---

## 6. Nodbusiness in Third Person

When writing about Nodbusiness (press releases, legal, external docs):
```
✅ "Nodbusiness is a global platform for businesses and communities."
✅ "Nodbusiness Technologies PLC" (full legal name in formal contexts)
❌ "The Nodbusiness platform" (drop "the" — it's a proper noun)
❌ "Our Nodbusiness" (redundant)
```

## 7. Language Support

**English** is the primary language.
**Amharic** is the first additional language.

When writing for both:
- English first, Amharic translation reviewed by a native speaker
- Amharic text follows the same voice and tone principles
- Do not use Google Translate for user-facing strings — use a professional translator
