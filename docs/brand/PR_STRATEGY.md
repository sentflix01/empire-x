```
Goal: 1,000 people know Nodbusiness exists before launch
Channels: Tech blogs, startup communities, social media

Target: Ethiopian tech community, African tech press, diaspora networks
```

### Phase 2 — Establish Authority
```
Goal: Nodbusiness is recognized as the leading business platform in East Africa
Channels: Business press, partnerships, awards
Message: "X businesses earning on Nodbusiness. Join them."
Target: Business press, investors, enterprise clients
```

### Phase 3 — Global Story
```
Goal: Nodbusiness is mentioned alongside Telegram, Shopify, WeChat as a platform
Channels: International tech press, conference speaking, research citations
Message: "The platform that democratized business for the next billion"
Target: International VCs, global tech press, policy makers
```

## 5. Crisis Communication Framework

### Severity Levels

**Level 1 — Minor (Internal + Affected Users)**
```
Examples: Brief downtime, delayed payments, minor data display error
Response time: Within 4 hours
Communication: Status page update + in-app notification
Do not: Escalate to press, do not post on social media
```

**Level 2 — Significant (All Users)**
```
Examples: Extended downtime (>2 hours), payment processing failure, data sync issue
Response time: Within 1 hour
Communication: Status page + email to all users + social media post
Tone: Transparent, factual, no excuses, clear ETA for resolution
```

**Level 3 — Crisis (External Impact)**
```
Examples: Data breach, security vulnerability, major fraud event, legal action
Response time: Within 30 minutes for internal, 2 hours for external statement
Communication: Press statement + user notification + regulatory notification (if GDPR applies)
Do: Engage legal counsel immediately, notify affected users, be transparent
Do not: Deny, minimize, deflect, or go silent
```

### Crisis Statement Template
```
[Date, Time]

We are aware of [specific issue] affecting [who is affected].

What happened: [Factual, plain-language description]

What we have done: [Actions taken so far]

What we are doing next: [Next steps with timeline]

What this means for you: [Specific impact on users]

We will provide an update by [specific time].

For questions: [contact email]

[Founder name]
CEO, Nodbusiness Technologies
```

## 6. Social Media Strategy

### Platform Priority
```
1. LinkedIn — B2B, investors, business owners, professional credibility
2. Twitter/X — Tech community, real-time updates, developer audience
3. Instagram — Visual brand, showroom success stories, community
4. TikTok — Product demos, founder story, younger business owners
5. Telegram — Community announcements (aligned with platform)
6. YouTube — Deep-dive tutorials, product demos, founder interviews
```

### Content Calendar Framework
```
Monday:    Product update or feature highlight
Tuesday:   User/business success story
Wednesday: Industry insight or opinion
Thursday:  Behind-the-scenes / team story
Friday:    Community engagement (poll, question, celebration)
Saturday:  Educational content (how-to, tip)
Sunday:    Off or repost user content
```

### Response Time SLAs
```
Direct mentions: 2 hours during business hours
DMs: 4 hours during business hours
Comments on posts: 24 hours
Negative reviews: 4 hours (high priority)
```
