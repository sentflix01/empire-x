## 1. What Is Valuation?

A company's valuation is the agreed-upon price of the entire company at a specific moment.
When you raise investment, you negotiate this number. It determines how much equity
you give away for the money you receive.

```
Basic formula:
  Post-money valuation = Pre-money valuation + Investment amount
  Equity sold (%) = Investment amount / Post-money valuation

Example:
  Pre-money valuation: $4,000,000
  Investment amount:   $1,000,000
  Post-money:          $5,000,000
  Equity sold:         $1M / $5M = 20%
```

## 2. How SaaS/Platform Companies Are Valued

There are three main methods investors use:

### Method 1 — Revenue Multiple (most common for growth-stage)

For SaaS and platform businesses, investors apply a multiple to Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):

```
Valuation = ARR × Revenue Multiple

Revenue multiples by stage (2024–2026 market):
  Pre-revenue startup:     0× (no revenue, valued on potential)
  Early revenue ($0-10K MRR): 10× – 30× ARR
  Traction ($10K-100K MRR): 15× – 40× ARR
  Scale ($100K+ MRR):      20× – 60× ARR
  (Multiples are lower for Africa/emerging market deals)

Nodbusiness projections:
  Phase 2 target: $25,000 MRR = $300,000 ARR
  Valuation at 15× ARR: $4,500,000
  Valuation at 20× ARR: $6,000,000
  → Realistic seed valuation range: $3M–$6M pre-money
```

### Method 2 — Comparable Transactions (comps)

Look at what similar companies sold for or raised at:

| Company | Type | Stage | Raise Amount | Valuation |
|---------|------|-------|-------------|-----------|
| Flutterwave (Nigeria) | Fintech platform | Series A | $10M | $60M |
| Paystack (Nigeria) | Payment platform | Seed | $1.3M | ~$6M |
| Andela (Nigeria) | Tech marketplace | Series A | $24M | $100M |
| Jumia (Pan-Africa) | E-commerce | Series A | $35M | ~$150M |
| African SaaS average | B2B SaaS | Seed | $500K–$2M | $3M–$10M |

Nodbusiness comparable: marketplace + SaaS hybrid in Africa
**Realistic seed valuation: $2M–$6M pre-money**

### Method 3 — Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)

For more mature companies. Project future cash flows and discount them to present value.
Not typically used at seed stage — too much uncertainty.

## 5. Unit Economics (What Investors Look For)

```
Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):
  Marketing spend per new paying customer
  Target: < $30 per paying business (organic/referral model)
  Current: Unknown (pre-launch)
  How to achieve low CAC: referral program, content marketing, SEO

Lifetime Value (LTV):
  Average revenue per customer × average subscription length
  At $29/month average and 24-month average lifetime:
  LTV = $29 × 24 = $696 per customer

LTV:CAC Ratio (investors want > 3:1):
  At $696 LTV and $30 CAC: LTV:CAC = 23:1 (excellent)
  At $696 LTV and $100 CAC: LTV:CAC = 7:1 (still strong)

Gross Margin (investors want 60–80% for SaaS):
  Revenue per customer: $29/month
  Hosting cost per customer: ~$2/month (Neon, Railway, Vercel)
  Support cost per customer: ~$1/month
  Gross margin: ($29 - $3) / $29 = 89.7% (excellent)
  
Monthly Churn (investors want < 5% for SaaS):
  Target: < 3% monthly churn (12-month retention of 70%)
  At 3% monthly churn: average customer life = 33 months
```

## 6. Dilution Table — Founder Ownership Over Time

```
Starting:
  Founder:  100%

After Pre-Seed ($100K at $500K pre-money valuation):
  Founder:   83.3%
  Investors: 16.7%
  ($100K / $600K post-money = 16.7%)

After Seed ($1M at $4M pre-money):
  Pre-dilution: Founder 83.3%
  New equity sold: 20% ($1M / $5M post)
  Founder post-seed: 83.3% × 80% = 66.7%
  Pre-seed investors: 16.7% × 80% = 13.3%
  Seed investors: 20%

After Series A ($4M at $12M pre-money):
  New equity sold: 25% ($4M / $16M post)
  Founder post-A: 66.7% × 75% = 50%
  (Still majority owner)

After Employee Option Pool (10% reserved for team):
  Usually created before each round (dilutes existing, not new investors)
  Founder post-A with pool: ~45%
```

**Goal:** Never drop below 50% before Series A. Below 50% = you lose practical control.

```

2. Sign a paying customer        → Even $100/month proves willingness to pay

5. Win a competition             → Seedstars, Slush, Demo Africa

8. Register trademark + company  → Shows seriousness
```
