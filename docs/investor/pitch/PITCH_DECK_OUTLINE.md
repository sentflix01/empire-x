# Nodbusiness — Pitch Deck Outline

> **Classification:** Confidential
> **Format:** 10–12 slides. Clear. Visual. No walls of text.
> **Rule:** Every slide answers one question an investor is thinking.

---

## Acronyms Used in This Document

| Acronym | Full Form |
|---------|-----------|
| MAU | Monthly Active Users — unique users active at least once per month |
| MRR | Monthly Recurring Revenue — total subscription revenue in one month |
| ARR | Annual Recurring Revenue — total subscription revenue over 12 months |
| TAM | Total Addressable Market — entire global market for the product |
| SAM | Serviceable Addressable Market — portion of TAM you can realistically reach |
| SOM | Serviceable Obtainable Market — realistic market share you can capture |
| CAC | Customer Acquisition Cost — total cost to acquire one paying customer |
| LTV | Lifetime Value — total revenue from one customer over their lifetime |
| SAFE | Simple Agreement for Future Equity — pre-seed investment instrument |
| VC | Venture Capital — professional investment firm |
| SMB | Small and Medium-sized Business |
| CAGR | Compound Annual Growth Rate |
| AI | Artificial Intelligence |
| UI | User Interface |

### Slide 9 — Go-To-Market Strategy

**Question investor is thinking:** "How will you get users and customers?"

```
Content:
  Headline: "Phase 1: Network effect engine"

  Channel 1: Business-led growth
    → Every showroom a business publishes is a landing page
    → Business shares link with their customers → customers join
    → 1 business brings average 50 customers to platform

  Channel 2: Referral program
    → Users refer others → both get rewards
    → Target: 1 user refers 1.5 others (viral coefficient 1.5)

  Channel 3: Creator partnerships
    → 50–100 content creators get Beta Pioneer access
    → Their content drives organic user acquisition
    → 0 paid marketing in Phase 1

  Phase 2 paid channels:
    → App store optimization (ASO)
    → LinkedIn ads targeting business owners
    → Google Ads targeting "website builder" + location keywords

Key message: Organic growth in Phase 1. Paid acquisition scales in Phase 2.
```

## Pitch Deck Design Rules

```
✅ Maximum 12 slides (most investors spend < 3 minutes on a deck)
✅ One key message per slide
✅ Large visuals, minimal text (< 30 words per slide)
✅ Consistent branding (Nodbusiness colors, font)
✅ High-resolution images and screenshots
✅ Footer on every slide: "Nodbusiness Technologies — Confidential"
✅ Page numbers on every slide

❌ No Comic Sans or Times New Roman
❌ No bullet points with 8+ items
❌ No charts without labels
❌ No jargon that needs explaining
❌ No promises you cannot substantiate with data
```
