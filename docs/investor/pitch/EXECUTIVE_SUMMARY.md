## NODBUSINESS — THE GLOBAL SUPERAPP FOR BUSINESS

**One line:** Nodbusiness lets any business in the world build a professional
showroom website in 10 minutes, sell products, and connect with customers —
all in one platform.

---

### The Problem

400 million small businesses worldwide have no professional digital presence.
Building a website costs $2,000–$10,000 and takes months. Existing platforms
(Shopify, Wix, Squarespace) require technical skill and don't integrate social
discovery, local payments, or community features that emerging market businesses need.

---

### The Solution

Nodbusiness is a three-in-one platform:
- **Showroom Builder** — Build a professional business website in 10 minutes, no code required
- **Social Feed** — Customers discover businesses through a social feed, nearby map, and search
- **Commerce Layer** — Sell products, accept payments (Stripe + local gateways), manage orders

---

### Business Model

- **Subscriptions:** Free → Starter ($19/mo) → Pro ($49/mo) → Business ($99/mo)
- **Template Marketplace:** Developers build templates, earn 70% revenue share (AI-priced)
- **Featured Placement:** Businesses pay for premium discovery visibility

---

### Traction

- Platform fully built: 45+ features, 252 backend tests passing, 0 TypeScript errors
- 3 provisional patents filed (AI pricing, revenue share system, discovery algorithm)
- Trademark filed in Ethiopia; USA + EU pending
- Registered company: Nodbusiness Technologies PLC

---

### Market

- $24B global SMB website builder + marketplace market
- $1.2B Africa + diaspora serviceable market, growing at 28% CAGR
- 150M+ SMBs in Africa with no digital presence

---

### Why Now

- Mobile internet adoption in Africa growing at 20% annually
- Digital payments infrastructure matured (M-Pesa, Telebirr, Flutterwave)
- First-mover window: no platform combines social + commerce + showroom in one

---

### Go-To-Market

Phase 1: Business-led organic growth — every published showroom brings the
business's customers onto the platform. Target: 10,000 MAU and 500 showrooms
before monetization.

Phase 2: Launch paid plans + developer template marketplace. Target: $25,000 MRR.
