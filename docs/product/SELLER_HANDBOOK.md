## Welcome to Empire X

Empire X is Ethiopia's super-app for business. In 30 minutes, you can have:
- A professional online store (Showroom)
- Products listed in the marketplace
- A way to accept digital payment from anywhere in Ethiopia
- An order management system
- A direct line of communication with your customers

## Getting Started: Your First 30 Minutes

### 1. Create your account (5 minutes)
1. Go to empirex.app → Register
2. Enter your email or phone number
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to you
4. Choose "Business Account" on the next screen

### 2. Complete your business profile (10 minutes)
- Business name and description
- Choose your category (Fashion, Food, Electronics, etc.)
- Upload your logo
- Add your contact details (phone, email, address)
- Set your theme colour (gold is popular!)

### 3. Create your Showroom (10 minutes)
- Go to Showroom Studio → Create Showroom
- Pick a template that matches your business
- Customize the sections (add your products, about us, contact)
- Publish your showroom
- Share your showroom link on WhatsApp and Telegram!

### 4. List your first product (5 minutes)
- Go to Marketplace → + List Product
- Add clear photos (natural light works best)
- Write a clear title and description
- Set a fair price in ETB
- Set your stock count
- Click "List"

## Product Listing Best Practices

### Photos
- **Use natural light** — step outside or near a window
- **Show multiple angles** — at least 3 photos per product
- **Clean background** — white cloth or plain wall
- **Show scale** — include a hand or familiar object for size reference
- **Avoid filters** — buyers want to see the real color

### Titles
- Be specific: "Habesha Kemis — White, Size M" (not just "Dress")
- Include key search terms: "Ethiopian Coffee — 500g Yirgacheffe Natural"
- Include size, colour, material where relevant

### Descriptions
- What is it made of?
- What are the dimensions?
- Who is it for?
- What problem does it solve?
- What's included in the package?

### Pricing
- Research what similar items sell for
- Factor in: material cost + your time + delivery margin
- Don't underprice — it devalues your work and attracts bargain hunters, not loyal customers
- You can offer discounts via Coupon Codes (see below)

## Accepting Payment

Empire X supports four ways to get paid:

| Method | How | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Chapa / Telebirr** | Buyer pays via Telebirr, CBE Birr, or M-Pesa | Ethiopian buyers |
| **Stripe (Card)** | Buyer pays with debit/credit card or digital wallet | International buyers or diaspora |
| **Cash on Delivery** | Buyer pays when they receive the item | Local delivery, trusted buyers |
| **Wallet** | Buyer pays from their Empire X wallet balance | Repeat customers |

**When do you get paid?**
- Stripe/Chapa: Payment arrives in your Empire X wallet within 24–48 hours of order confirmation
- COD: You collect cash on delivery — then mark order "Completed" in the app
- Commission (platform fee) is automatically deducted when you mark an order "Completed"

**How to withdraw your earnings:**
1. Go to Wallet → Withdraw
2. Enter the amount and your bank account details
3. Request is processed within 1–3 business days

## Managing Orders

### Order statuses
| Status | Meaning | Your action |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Order placed, payment not yet confirmed | Wait for payment, or contact buyer |
| **Paid** | Payment confirmed | Start preparing the order → Mark "Processing" |
| **Processing** | You're preparing/shipping | Update delivery tracking, ship item |
| **Completed** | Delivered and confirmed | No action needed — commission collected |
| **Cancelled** | Order cancelled | No action needed |

### Delivery tracking
When you ship an order:
1. Go to Orders → click the order → expand tracking section
2. Select the delivery stage (Packed / Picked Up / In Transit / etc.)
3. Add optional note and location
4. Buyers can see this in real-time

## Coupon Codes

Create discount codes to reward loyal customers or run promotions.

### How to create a coupon
1. Go to Menu → My Coupons
2. Click "New Coupon"
3. Set the code (or click Generate)
4. Choose type: percentage off (20%) or fixed amount (ETB 50 off)
5. Optionally set: minimum order value, usage limit, expiry date
6. Share the code with your customers

### Best practices
- Use coupons for: first-order discounts, seasonal promotions, rewarding repeat customers
- Set a usage limit to prevent unlimited redemption
- Announce coupons on your feed posts to drive urgency

## Growing Your Customer Base

### 1. Post regularly on the feed
- Share your products, behind-the-scenes, and customer stories
- Post at least 3 times per week
- Use hashtags like #EthiopianFashion, #AddisAbeba, #HabeshaKemis

### 2. Use your Showroom link
- Add it to your WhatsApp Business description
- Share it in relevant Telegram groups
- Put it in your Instagram/TikTok bio

### 3. Ask for reviews
- After every successful order, send a message: "I hope you love your order! Would you mind leaving a review?"
- Good reviews make new buyers trust you

### 4. Referral program
- Share your referral link → both you and your customer earn bonus points
- Points can be used for discounts and featured listing boosts

### 5. Offer coupons for first orders
- "Use code FIRST20 for 20% off your first order" in your showroom description

## What You Can and Cannot Sell

### ✅ Allowed
- Traditional Ethiopian clothing, textiles, jewelry
- Food products (packaged, sealed)
- Coffee, spices, grains
- Electronics (new or clearly described condition)
- Handmade crafts
- Services (consulting, tutoring, etc.)
- Digital products (with seller's consent)

### ❌ Not allowed
- Counterfeit or brand-infringing products
- Prescription medications
- Weapons, firearms, or ammunition
- Alcoholic beverages (without license)
- Wildlife products or endangered species
- Stolen goods
- Adult content

For the full list: see `docs/legal/CONTENT_POLICY.md`

## Commission Structure

| Order value | Commission rate |
|---|---|
| ETB 0 – 1,000 | 6% |
| ETB 1,001 – 5,000 | 5% |
| ETB 5,001+ | 4% |
| Pro subscribers (any value) | 4% |

Commission is automatically deducted from your wallet when an order is marked "Completed."
You only pay commission on completed sales — never on listings or pending orders.
