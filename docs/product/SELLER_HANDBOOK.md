- A way to accept digital payment from customers anywhere in the world

## Getting Started: Your First 30 Minutes

### 1. Create your account (5 minutes)
1. Go to nodbusiness.vercel.app → Register
2. Enter your email or phone number
3. Enter the 6-digit OTP code sent to you
4. Choose "Business Account" on the next screen

### 2. Complete your business profile (10 minutes)
- Business name and description
- Choose your category (Fashion, Food, Electronics, Services, etc.)
- Upload your logo or profile photo
- Add your contact details (phone, email, address/city)
- Set your business theme color

### 3. Create your Showroom (10 minutes)
- Go to Showroom Studio → Create Showroom
- Pick a template that matches your business type
- Customize sections (products, about us, contact info, gallery)
- Publish your showroom
- Share your showroom link on your social channels and messaging apps

### 4. List your first product (5 minutes)
- Go to Marketplace → + List Product
- Add clear photos (natural light works best)
- Write a clear title and description
- Set a fair price in your preferred currency
- Set your stock count
- Click "List"

## Product Listing Best Practices

### Photos
- **Use natural light** — step outside or near a window
- **Show multiple angles** — at least 3 photos per product
- **Clean background** — white cloth, wall, or plain surface
- **Show scale** — include a hand or familiar object for size reference
- **Avoid heavy filters** — buyers want to see the real color and quality

### Titles
Be specific and searchable:
- ✅ "Handmade Leather Wallet — Brown, Full-Grain"
- ✅ "Ethiopian Coffee — 500g Yirgacheffe Natural Process"
- ✅ "Traditional Woven Basket — 30cm Diameter"
- ❌ "Nice bag" or "Coffee"

Include: material, size/weight, color, style, and key descriptors.

### Descriptions
Answer these questions:
- What is it made of?
- What are the dimensions or weight?
- Who is it for?
- What problem does it solve?
- What's included in the package?
- What should the buyer expect for delivery?

### Pricing
- Research what similar items sell for globally and locally
- Factor in: material cost + your time + packaging + delivery margin
- Don't underprice — it devalues your work and attracts bargain hunters instead of loyal customers
- You can offer discounts via Coupon Codes (see below)
- Show prices in local currency; buyers will see the equivalent in their currency

## Accepting Payment

Nodbusiness supports multiple payment methods. Available methods depend on your country and buyer's location.

| Method | How | Best for |
|--------|-----|---------|
| **Card (Stripe)** | Buyer pays with debit/credit card or digital wallet | International buyers, diaspora customers |
| **Mobile Money** | Buyer pays via local mobile payment app | Local buyers (M-Pesa, Telebirr, MTN Mobile Money, etc.) |
| **Cash on Delivery (COD)** | Buyer pays when they receive the item | Local delivery, buyers who don't have digital payment |
| **Wallet** | Buyer pays from their Nodbusiness wallet balance | Repeat customers |

**Reference (Ethiopia):** Chapa supports Telebirr, CBE Birr, M-Pesa, and all major Ethiopian bank transfers.

### When do you get paid?
- Card/Mobile Money: Payment arrives in your Nodbusiness wallet within 24–48 hours of order confirmation
- COD: You collect payment on delivery — then mark the order "Completed" in the app
- Platform commission is automatically deducted when you mark an order "Completed"

### How to withdraw your earnings
1. Go to Wallet → Withdraw
2. Enter the amount and your bank account or mobile money details
3. Withdrawal is processed within 1–3 business days

## Managing Orders

### Order Status Guide

| Status | Meaning | Your action |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Pending** | Order placed, payment not yet confirmed | Wait for payment, or contact buyer |
| **Paid** | Payment confirmed | Start preparing the order → Mark "Processing" |
| **Processing** | You are preparing/shipping | Update delivery tracking, ship item |
| **Completed** | Delivered and confirmed | No action needed — commission collected |
| **Cancelled** | Order cancelled | No action needed |

### Delivery Tracking
When you ship an order:
1. Go to Orders → click the order → expand tracking section
2. Select the delivery stage: Packed → Picked Up → In Transit → Out for Delivery → Delivered
3. Add optional note (e.g., tracking number, courier name)
4. Buyers see this in real time via the Orders page and push notifications

## Coupon Codes

Create discount codes to reward loyal customers or run promotions.

### How to create a coupon
1. Go to Menu → My Coupons → New Coupon
2. Enter a code (or click "Generate")
3. Choose type: percentage off (e.g., 20% off) or fixed amount (e.g., $10 off)
4. Optionally set: minimum order value, usage limit, expiry date
5. Share the code with your customers

### Best practices
- First-order coupons convert hesitant buyers: "FIRST20 for 20% off your first order"
- Set a usage limit to prevent unlimited redemption
- Announce coupons on your feed posts to create urgency

## Growing Your Customer Base

### 1. Post regularly on the feed
- Share products, behind-the-scenes content, and customer stories
- Post at least 3 times per week
- Use relevant hashtags for your product category and location

### 2. Share your Showroom link everywhere
- Add it to your WhatsApp Business description
- Share it in relevant community groups (Telegram, WhatsApp, Facebook)
- Put it in your Instagram/TikTok bio
- Include it in email signatures

### 3. Ask for reviews
After every successful delivery, message your buyer:
> "I hope you love your order! If you're happy, a quick review really helps. Thank you!"

Good reviews are your most powerful marketing tool.

### 4. Referral program
- Share your referral link → both you and the person you refer earn bonus points
- Points can be used for discounts and featured listing boosts

### 5. Respond to messages quickly
- Buyers who get a reply within 30 minutes are 5× more likely to purchase
- Set up push notifications so you never miss an inquiry

## What You Can and Cannot Sell

### ✅ Allowed
- Clothing, textiles, fashion accessories
- Food products (packaged and clearly labeled)
- Specialty foods (e.g., Ethiopian coffee, spices, imported goods)
- Electronics (new or clearly described condition, not stolen)
- Handmade crafts, artwork, and jewelry
- Services (consulting, tutoring, photography, etc.)
- Digital products (with appropriate licensing)
- Traditional and cultural products

### ❌ Not allowed
- Counterfeit or brand-infringing products
- Prescription medications
- Weapons, firearms, or ammunition
- Illegal drugs or controlled substances
- Wildlife products or endangered species
- Stolen goods of any kind
- Adult content
- Products requiring a license you don't hold

For the full list: see `docs/legal/CONTENT_POLICY.md` and `docs/trust-safety/COMMUNITY_STANDARDS.md`

## Commission Structure

Commission is charged as a percentage of the order total when an order is marked "Completed." You only pay commission on completed sales — never on listings or pending orders.

| Item | Rate |
|------|------|
| **Platform commission** | **Flat 3%** of the total paid amount (after discounts, before delivery) |
| Payment processing | Charged by Stripe/Chapa, not by Nodbusiness |

> Tiered commission rates may be introduced in the future. If they are, this page and the
> Seller Agreement will be updated before any change takes effect — you will always see the
> exact rate at the time of sale.

**Payouts:** After an order completes, net proceeds (order total minus 3%) are settled to your
Nodbusiness wallet, from which you can request withdrawal to your bank ("Wallet → Withdraw",
processed within 1–3 business days). During the launch period settlement is initiated by
Nodbusiness after completion rather than automatically at payment time.
