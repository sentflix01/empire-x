## 1. Why File Patents

Patent protection for Halehame serves three purposes:

1. **Offense** — Stop competitors from copying and commercializing your specific
   technical methods (AI pricing, revenue share algorithm, discovery method).

2. **Defense** — If a large competitor (Shopify, Wix) sues you for patent
   infringement, having your own patents gives you leverage to cross-license
   or negotiate rather than simply surrendering.

3. **Valuation** — Investors and acquirers pay significantly more for companies
   with granted patents. A patent portfolio signals defensible technology.

## 2. What Is Patentable at Halehame

### Rule: Patents protect methods and systems, not ideas.

The question is not "is this a good idea?" but "does this describe a specific
technical process that is new and non-obvious?"

### Patentable Inventions Identified

**Patent 1 — AI Dynamic Template Pricing Method**
The system continuously monitors demand signals (view count, install rate, retention)
combined with quality signals (user ratings, accessibility scores) and a competition
factor (how many businesses exist in the same category), then applies a weighted
algorithm to adjust template prices automatically without manual intervention.

*Why it's patentable:*
No existing platform dynamically prices digital templates based on simultaneous
analysis of demand, quality, and competitive density. Shopify, Wix, and Squarespace
all use fixed pricing. This method is novel and non-obvious.

**Patent 2 — Developer Revenue Share Quality-Adjustment System**
A system for calculating and distributing revenue in a template marketplace where
a developer's revenue share percentage is dynamically adjusted based on a quality
score derived from user ratings, accessibility compliance, performance metrics,
and retention rate — rather than a fixed split.

*Why it's patentable:*
Apple App Store, Google Play, and Shopify App Store all use fixed revenue splits
(70/30 or 85/15). Quality-adjusted dynamic revenue sharing has not been
implemented or patented by any major marketplace.

**Patent 3 — Combined Social-Commerce Showroom Discovery Method**
A method for ranking and presenting business showrooms to users that combines:
(a) social graph signals (who the user follows and their engagement patterns),
(b) geographic proximity (GPS distance to business),
(c) category taxonomy matching (business type aligned to user interest history), and
(d) real-time trending signals from the social feed —
into a single unified discovery score.

*Why it's patentable:*
No platform currently combines all four signals into a unified discovery algorithm
for business showrooms. Google Maps uses proximity. LinkedIn uses social graph.
Shopify has no social layer. The four-factor combination is novel.

## 3. What Is NOT Patentable

Avoid wasting money on these:

| Claim | Why Not Patentable |
|-------|-------------------|
| "A platform combining social and commerce" | Too broad, abstract idea |
| "A showroom builder with templates" | Functional feature, already exists conceptually |
| "A marketplace where developers earn revenue" | Abstract business method |
| "AI-generated product descriptions" | OpenAI, Google, and others already patented AI content generation broadly |
| "A wallet for digital payments" | Mobile payment patents are saturated |

## 4. Filing Strategy

### Phase 1 — Before Launch (DO THIS FIRST)

**File: 3 Provisional Patent Applications at USPTO**

A provisional patent application:
- Establishes your priority date immediately
- Costs $320 (micro entity) per application
- Gives you "Patent Pending" status
- Gives you 12 months to file the full utility application
- Does NOT require claims (just a description)
- Easier and faster to draft than a full application

```
Cost estimate for 3 provisionals (with attorney):
  → Attorney drafting: $1,500–3,000 each
  → USPTO fees: $320 each (micro entity rate)
  → Total: $5,500–10,000 for all 3
  → Timeline: Can be filed within 2–4 weeks
```

**Critical:** File BEFORE any public launch, demo, investor pitch, or press coverage.
Under US patent law (35 USC § 102), public disclosure starts a 1-year clock.
After 12 months of public disclosure without a patent filing, you LOSE patent rights.

### Phase 2 — After Launch / When Funded

**Upgrade to Full Utility Patents**

Within 12 months of the provisional filing:
- File full utility patent applications (or PCT international application)
- Must include: detailed description, drawings, and formal claims
- Claims define the exact scope of your protection
- Requires a registered patent attorney (USPTO requires this)

```
Cost estimate for 3 full utility patents:
  → Attorney: $8,000–15,000 each
  → USPTO fees: $1,600–3,000 each
  → Total: $30,000–55,000 for all 3
  → Timeline: 2–3 years to grant
```

### Phase 3 — International Protection

After US utility patents are filed, consider:

**PCT Application (Patent Cooperation Treaty)**
- One application filed at WIPO covers 157 countries
- Gives you 30 months to decide which countries to pursue nationally
- Cost: $3,000–5,000 + $1,500–3,000 per country national phase entry

**Priority Countries for Halehame:**
1. USA (USPTO) — already covered in Phase 1/2
2. Ethiopia (EIPO) — home market
3. Kenya (KIPI) — East Africa gateway
4. Nigeria (Patents & Designs Registry) — West Africa largest market
5. European Union (via EPO) — unified coverage for 38 countries
6. India (Patent Office, Mumbai) — 1.4 billion people

## 5. Trade Secret Strategy (Alternative/Complement to Patents)

For the AI pricing algorithm specifically, trade secret protection may be MORE
valuable than a patent because:

| Factor | Patent | Trade Secret |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Duration | 20 years from filing | Forever (as long as kept secret) |
| Cost | $30,000–55,000 | $0 (just NDA + access control) |
| Disclosure | Must disclose publicly when granted | Never disclosed |
| Coverage | Only in countries where filed | Global (through contract law) |
| Risk | Expires. Competitors can design around it. | Risk: independent discovery |

**Decision:** Use BOTH.
- File provisional patent (establishes priority, "Patent Pending" signal)
- Simultaneously protect as trade secret (limit access, NDA everyone)
- If competitor independently creates the same algorithm, patent wins.
- If algorithm stays secret, trade secret protection is perpetual.

## 8. Finding a Patent Attorney

Requirements for your patent attorney:
- **Must be registered with USPTO** (only registered patent agents/attorneys can file)
- Experience with **software/method patents** specifically
- Ideally has filed patents for marketplace or platform companies
- International filing experience (PCT, EPO)

Resources to find one:
- USPTO Attorney/Agent Search: www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/patent-and-trademark-practitioners
- Ask your IP attorney for referral to a patent specialist
- LinkedIn: search "patent attorney software USPTO"
- Martindale-Hubbell directory: www.martindale.com

Expected cost for initial consultation: $300–500 (1–2 hours).
Get quotes from at least 3 attorneys before engaging one.
