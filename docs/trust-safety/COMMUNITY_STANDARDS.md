> **The rules that govern what is allowed on the Nodbusiness platform.**
> These standards apply to all users, businesses, and developers on the platform, in every country where Nodbusiness operates.

## Our Commitment

Nodbusiness is built on the belief that business and community are better when people can trust each other. These standards exist to protect that trust — for individual users, for business owners, and for the global community.

We want Nodbusiness to be a place where:
- Businesses can build a trusted presence without fear of fake competition
- Buyers can purchase with confidence
- People can connect authentically
- Everyone is treated with dignity

We enforce these standards consistently and fairly. We do not apply them selectively based on who you are, where you are from, or what your political views are.

---

## 1. Safety

### 1.1 Violence and Physical Safety
**Not allowed:**
- Content that directly threatens violence against a specific person or group
- Instructions for committing violence (e.g., how to make weapons, bombs)
- Glorification or celebration of violent acts or perpetrators
- Incitement — encouraging others to commit violence

**Allowed:**
- News reporting on violent events
- Historical documentation
- Discussions of self-defense
- Fiction that involves violence but does not glorify it

### 1.2 Child Safety
**Strictly prohibited — zero tolerance:**
- CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material) of any kind
- Grooming behavior toward minors
- Content that sexualizes people under 18
- Facilitating trafficking of minors

Any CSAM discovered is immediately reported to NCMEC (National Center for Missing and Exploited Children) and relevant authorities. No appeal is available for accounts involved in CSAM violations.

### 1.3 Self-Harm and Suicide
**Not allowed:**
- Content that instructs on methods of self-harm or suicide
- Content that encourages or glorifies suicide or self-harm

**Allowed:**
- Raising awareness about mental health
- Personal stories of recovery (handled with care)
- Resources and support information

If you or someone you know is in crisis, please reach out to a local emergency service or a crisis line in your country.

---

## 2. Authenticity

### 2.1 Real Identity
- You may not impersonate another person, brand, organization, or public figure
- Parody and satire accounts are allowed but must clearly identify themselves as such
- You may not create fake accounts to deceive other users

### 2.2 Misinformation
- Do not knowingly share false information that could harm people
- Do not share medical misinformation (fake cures, dangerous treatments)
- Do not share false information about elections or civic processes

We do not remove opinions or content just because it is controversial. We remove content that is demonstrably false and causes real-world harm.

### 2.3 Fake Reviews and Ratings
- You may not write reviews for your own business
- You may not pay others to write fake positive reviews
- You may not organize campaigns to give businesses unfair negative reviews
- Reviews must reflect genuine experiences

---

## 3. Respect and Dignity

### 3.1 Hate Speech
**Not allowed:**
- Content that attacks people based on: race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, caste
- Dehumanizing language targeting any protected group
- Calls for discrimination against protected groups

**Allowed:**
- Discussion of discrimination (condemning it)
- Personal sharing of experiences with discrimination
- Religious or cultural expression that does not attack other groups

### 3.2 Harassment and Bullying
**Not allowed:**
- Targeted harassment of an individual (repeated unwanted contact, threats, coordinated attacks)
- Sharing someone's personal information to expose or harm them ("doxxing")
- Content designed to shame or humiliate an individual
- Sending unsolicited explicit messages

**Allowed:**
- Criticism of public figures in their public role
- Reviews that are honest but harsh
- Disagreement and debate (done respectfully)

### 3.3 Hate Organizations
Organizations that promote hatred, terrorism, or violence are not allowed on Nodbusiness in any capacity — as users, businesses, or advertisers.

---

## 4. Privacy

### 4.1 Personal Information
- Do not share another person's private information without their consent
- This includes: home addresses, phone numbers, personal photos, financial information, medical information
- Screenshots of private conversations shared to harass are not allowed

### 4.2 Photography and Recording
- Do not post identifiable photos or videos of other people without their consent
- Exception: public figures in their public roles, public events where recording is expected

## 5. Marketplace Rules

### 5.1 Prohibited Products and Services
The following may NOT be listed or sold on Nodbusiness under any circumstances:

| Category | Examples |
|---------|---------|
| Weapons | Firearms, ammunition, knives marketed as weapons, weapon components |
| Dangerous goods | Explosives, flammable chemicals, toxic substances |
| Drugs | Illegal narcotics, prescription drugs without prescription |
| Counterfeit goods | Fake brand items, pirated software |
| Stolen goods | Items obtained through theft |
| Wildlife | Endangered species, ivory, illegally captured animals |
| Human trafficking | Any facilitation of trafficking, forced labor |
| Regulated financial products | Securities, unlicensed financial advice |
| Adult content | Explicitly sexual content (unless platform expands to adults-only section with proper controls) |

### 5.2 Honest Listings
- Product descriptions must accurately describe what is being sold
- Photos must show the actual product being sold
- Prices must include all fees (no hidden charges revealed at checkout)
- Stock availability must be accurate

### 5.3 Seller Responsibilities
- Deliver what was sold, within the time stated
- Respond to buyer messages within 48 hours
- Accept returns where legally required in the buyer's country
- Do not engage in price fixing with other sellers

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

### 6.1 Copyright
- Only post content you created or have permission to share
- Do not reproduce significant portions of copyrighted works without permission
- We respond to valid DMCA takedown notices

### 6.2 Trademarks
- Do not use another company's trademark in a way that causes confusion
- Do not list counterfeit products using real brand names

### 6.3 AI-Generated Content
- You may use AI to help create content on Nodbusiness
- You must not use AI to generate fake reviews, fake business listings, or impersonate real people

---

## 7. Platform Integrity

### 7.1 Spam
- Do not send mass unsolicited messages
- Do not post repetitive content across many listings or posts
- Do not use automation to artificially inflate engagement (likes, follows, views)

### 7.2 Manipulation
- Do not use fake accounts to manipulate reviews, polls, or rankings
- Do not artificially boost content through paid engagement not disclosed as advertising
- Do not exploit platform features to gain unfair advantage over other sellers

### 7.3 Scams
- Do not attempt to deceive users out of money
- Do not create fake urgency to pressure purchases (fake timers, fake "limited stock")
- Do not use advance fee fraud tactics

### 7.4 Developer Rules (for third-party integrators)
- Apps and integrations must follow these Community Standards
- You may not use Nodbusiness APIs to scrape data for other platforms
- You may not use API access to spam or harm users
- Data collected via API may only be used for the disclosed purpose

---

## 8. Enforcement

We take the following actions depending on the severity and history of violations:

| Action | When Applied |
|--------|-------------|
| Content removed | Any content violating these standards |
| Warning issued | First or minor violations |
| Feature restriction | Repeat minor violations |
| Temporary suspension | Serious or repeat violations |
| Permanent termination | Severe violations, repeated behavior, CSAM |
| Legal referral | CSAM, credible violence threats, financial crimes |

### Appeals
If you believe we made a mistake, you can appeal through the in-app process. See our full appeals process in the Trust & Safety Framework document.

### What We Don't Remove
We do not remove content simply because:
- It is critical of Nodbusiness
- It is controversial or unpopular
- Someone disagrees with it
- It is politically sensitive (without specific rule violations)

---

## 9. Reporting Violations

If you see a violation:
- Use the "Report" button on any content, profile, or listing
- Email us at: safety@nodbusiness.com
- For urgent child safety issues: safety@nodbusiness.com (flagged as URGENT)

We investigate every report. We do not tolerate false reporting to harm competitors.
