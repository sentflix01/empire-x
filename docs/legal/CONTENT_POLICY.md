# Nodbusiness — Content Policy

> The rules that govern what is and isn't allowed on Nodbusiness.
> This applies to all users, sellers, and business accounts.
> **Last updated:** August 2026

---

## Prohibited Content — Zero Tolerance (Immediate Ban)

### Child Safety
- Any sexual content involving minors (CSAM)
- Content that grooms, exploits, or endangers children
- This will be reported to relevant Ethiopian authorities and international bodies (e.g., NCMEC)

### Terrorism & Violence
- Content that promotes, glorifies, or recruits for terrorist organizations
- Threats of physical violence against specific individuals or groups
- Content that incites ethnic, religious, or political violence
- **Relevant law:** Ethiopian Anti-Terrorism Proclamation No. 652/2009

### Hate Speech
- Content that dehumanizes people based on ethnicity, religion, nationality, disability, gender, or sexual orientation
- Ethiopia has over 80 ethnic groups — content that incites ethnic hatred is especially prohibited

### Fraud & Deception
- Selling counterfeit products
- Impersonating government agencies, NGOs, banks, or other platforms
- Phishing links or fake login pages
- Pyramid schemes, Ponzi schemes, or fraudulent investment offers

### Non-Consensual Content
- Sharing intimate images without consent ("revenge porn")
- Doxxing (publishing private personal information)

---

## Restricted Content (Allowed With Conditions)

### Alcohol & Tobacco
- Listings are allowed only where the seller has appropriate licenses
- Must not target users under 18
- No delivery to minors — seller is responsible for age verification

### Financial Services
- Investment advice must include appropriate disclaimers
- No unlicensed forex trading or cryptocurrency exchange promotion
- **Relevant regulation:** National Bank of Ethiopia directives

### Medical & Health
- No prescription drug sales without a licensed pharmacy license
- Medical advice must recommend professional consultation
- No unproven miracle cures or false health claims

### Political Content
- Political campaign content is allowed if clearly labeled as such
- No coordinated inauthentic behavior (fake accounts, bot networks)
- No vote suppression or election interference content
- **Relevant law:** Ethiopian Electoral Law

### Adult Content
- No explicit sexual content visible to the general feed
- Adult products (e.g., lingerie) may be listed in Marketplace with age-appropriate labeling

---

## Commercial Standards

### Product Listings
- Products must be accurately described (title, condition, price, images)
- Prices must be in ETB (other currencies may be shown as reference only)
- In-stock status must be accurate — selling items you don't have is fraud
- Prohibited items: weapons, drugs, wildlife products, counterfeit goods, stolen items

### Reviews
- Must be based on a genuine transaction
- No incentivized reviews without disclosure
- No fake reviews (buying, selling, or trading reviews)
- Sellers may not delete legitimate negative reviews

### Advertising / Promoted Posts
- Must be clearly labeled as "Sponsored" or "Ad"
- Must comply with all product and content policies
- No deceptive ad formats designed to look like organic content
- Targeting based on protected characteristics is prohibited

---

## Platform Integrity

- **One account per person.** Multiple accounts to evade bans are prohibited.
- **No automation.** Bots, scrapers, or automated posting without API access authorization.
- **No platform manipulation.** Artificially inflating likes, followers, or sales figures.

---

## Enforcement

| Violation | Action |
|---|---|
| First minor violation | Warning + content removal |
| Repeated minor violations | 7-day suspension |
| Serious violation (fraud, harassment) | Permanent ban + possible legal referral |
| CSAM or terrorism | Immediate permanent ban + mandatory legal reporting |

Appeals: appeals@nodbusiness.app (response within 7 business days)

---

## Reporting

To report a violation: use the "Report" button on any post, product, or profile.
Urgent reports (CSAM, threats of violence): safety@nodbusiness.app — monitored 24/7

---

## Compliance with Ethiopian Law

Nodbusiness operates under Ethiopian law, including:
- Computer Crime Proclamation No. 958/2016
- Mass Media and Freedom of Information Proclamation No. 590/2008
- Anti-Terrorism Proclamation No. 652/2009
- Ethiopian Broadcasting Authority (EBA) regulations
- National Bank of Ethiopia financial services regulations
- Ethiopian Intellectual Property Office (EIPO) copyright rules
