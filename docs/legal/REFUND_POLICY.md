## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds are issued for purchases made through the Nodbusiness Platform ("Platform").

**Platform Operator:** Nodbusiness Technologies PLC  
**Contact:** refunds@nodbusiness.app

## 2. Subscription Refunds

### 2.1 Paid Plans (Starter, Pro, Business, Enterprise)

| Scenario | Refund Eligibility | Amount |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| **Cancellation within 14 days of purchase/renewal** | Full refund | 100% (pro-rata unused) |
| **Cancellation after 14 days** | No refund (service provided) | 0% |
| **Downgrade mid-cycle** | Pro-rata credit to Wallet | Pro-rata difference |
| **Service outage > SLA** | Pro-rata credit per SLA | Per SLA terms |
| **Account termination by us (non-breach)** | Pro-rata refund | Pro-rata unused |

### 2.2 Free Plan
- **No refunds** (no payment made)
- Upgrade → paid plan terms apply

### 2.3 Refund Method
- **Wallet credit** (instant, usable for future payments)
- **Original payment method** (on request, 5-10 business days)
- **Wallet balance** withdrawable per withdrawal policy

## 3. Marketplace Refunds (Templates, Widgets, Integrations)

### 3.1 Digital Goods (Templates, Widgets)

| Scenario | Refund Eligibility | Conditions |
|----------|-------------------|------------|
| **Defective/Not as described** | Full refund | Within 7 days; developer notified; evidence required |
| **Compatibility issue** | Full refund | Within 7 days; not compatible with stated platform version |
| **Duplicate purchase** | Full refund | Within 24 hours; same item, same account |
| **Change of mind** | No refund | Digital goods non-returnable |
| **Developer removes listing** | Pro-rata refund | If subscription-based; within 30 days of removal |

### 3.2 Refund Process (Marketplace)
1. **Request:** Buyer clicks "Request Refund" on order page within eligibility window
2. **Notification:** Developer notified (48h to respond)
3. **Mediation:** If disputed, Nodbusiness mediates (72h)
4. **Decision:** Based on evidence (screenshots, logs, compatibility)
5. **Refund:** To Wallet (instant) or original method (5-10 days)

### 3.3 Developer Obligations
- **Accurate descriptions:** Features, compatibility, requirements
- **Support:** Documented support level on listing
- **Updates:** Minor updates free; major versions may be separate purchase
- **Compliance:** Refund policy adherence = marketplace participation requirement

## 4. Transaction Refunds (Orders, Payments)

### 4.1 Customer-Initiated (Buyer)

| Payment Method | Refund Window | Process | Timeline |
|----------------|---------------|---------|----------|
| **Stripe (Cards/Wallets)** | 180 days | Seller initiates → Stripe refund | 5-10 business days |
| **Chapa (ETB)** | 90 days | Seller initiates → Chapa refund | 3-7 business days |
| **Flutterwave/M-Pesa** | 90 days | Seller initiates → Provider refund | 3-7 business days |
| **Wallet** | 30 days | Instant Wallet credit | Instant |
| **COD** | N/A (cash) | Seller returns cash / Wallet credit | Per agreement |

### 4.2 Seller-Initiated (Full/Partial Refund)

| Scenario | Process | Timeline |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Order cancelled (pre-shipment)** | Seller cancels → auto-refund | Instant (Wallet) / 3-10 days (card) |
| **Partial refund (damage, missing items)** | Seller enters amount → refund | Same as above |
| **Return received** | Seller confirms → refund | Same as above |

### 4.3 Platform Commission on Refunds
- **Commission refunded** pro-rata on full refunds
- **Commission retained** on partial refunds (platform facilitated transaction)
- **Payment gateway fees** non-refundable (passed through from provider)

## 5. Wallet Refunds & Withdrawals

### 5.1 Wallet Balance Refunds
- **Eligible:** Wallet balance from refunds, overpayments, promotional credits
- **Method:** Withdrawal to verified bank account / mobile money
- **KYC Required:** Yes (per AML regulations)
- **Timeline:** 1-3 business days after verification
- **Limits:** Per jurisdiction (configurable)

### 5.2 Non-Refundable Wallet Funds
- **Promotional credits** (expire per terms)
- **Expired credits** (per promotion terms)
- **Funds from prohibited activity** (frozen, reported)

## 6. Dispute Resolution

### 6.1 Buyer-Seller Disputes
1. **Direct communication** (Platform messaging) — 48 hours
2. **Platform mediation** — 72 hours after escalation
3. **Decision** — Based on evidence (tracking, photos, messages, policy)
4. **Refund** — Executed per decision

### 6.2 Chargebacks (Stripe/Chapa/Flutterwave)
- **Seller notified** immediately
- **Evidence required** (tracking, delivery proof, communication)
- **Platform assists** with evidence compilation
- **Outcome:** Per payment provider rules; platform fee may apply if seller loses

### 6.3 Fraudulent Transactions
- **Immediate hold** on funds
- **Investigation** (72 hours)
- **Refund to buyer** if fraud confirmed
- **Seller account** reviewed for termination

## 8. Refund Request Process

### 8.1 How to Request
1. **Dashboard:** Orders → Select order → "Request Refund"
2. **Email:** refunds@nodbusiness.app (include order ID, reason, evidence)

### 8.2 Required Information
- Order ID / Template ID / Subscription ID
- Reason (dropdown + free text)
- Evidence (photos, screenshots, tracking, communication logs)
- Desired outcome (full/partial refund, Wallet credit, replacement)

### 8.3 Timeline
| Step | SLA |
|------|-----|
| **Acknowledgment** | 24 hours |
| **Initial Review** | 48 hours |
| **Mediation (if needed)** | 72 hours |
| **Decision** | 5 business days |
| **Refund Execution** | Per payment method (instant to 10 days) |
