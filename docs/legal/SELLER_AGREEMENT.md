# Nodbusiness — Seller Agreement (Draft)

> The contractual relationship between Nodbusiness and sellers on the platform.
> **Status:** Internal draft — requires legal review before publication.
> **Last updated:** August 2026

---

## 1. Who This Agreement Is Between

This agreement is between **Nodbusiness** ("the platform") and you, the **seller** ("you"), when you list products or services for sale on Nodbusiness.

By clicking "Start Selling" or listing your first product, you agree to these terms.

---

## 2. Seller Eligibility

You may sell on Nodbusiness if:
- You are 18 or older (or have guardian consent if 16–17 with supervisor)
- You have a valid Ethiopian phone number or email address
- You are selling items you own or have authority to sell
- Your products comply with the Content Policy and Ethiopian law

---

## 3. What You Can Sell

You must only list:
- Items you legally own or have authorization to sell
- Accurately described items (condition, size, material, age)
- Items that comply with the Content Policy

You are solely responsible for product legality in your jurisdiction. Nodbusiness is a platform, not a seller.

---

## 4. Pricing & Currency

- All prices must be listed in **Ethiopian Birr (ETB)**
- You may show prices in other currencies as reference but ETB is binding
- You may change prices at any time, but changes do not apply to existing orders
- You may not price products at ETB 0 (except for free services marked as free)

---

## 5. Order Fulfillment Obligations

When a buyer places an order, you must:

| Timeline | Action |
|---|---|
| Within 24 hours | Confirm the order or notify buyer of stock issue |
| Within 48 hours | Mark as "Processing" and begin preparation |
| Within agreed delivery window | Deliver or arrange pickup |
| Within 72 hours of delivery | Update order status to "Completed" |

**Failure to fulfill orders** (2+ unfulfilled orders in 30 days) may result in:
- Warning notification
- Temporary suspension of new orders
- Permanent removal from the marketplace

---

## 6. Commission & Fees

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Platform commission** | **Flat 3%** of the total paid order amount |
| Tiered / subscription discounts | Not currently offered; if introduced they will be published here and in the Seller Handbook before taking effect |
| Payment processing | Charged by the payment provider (Stripe/Chapa), not by Nodbusiness |

**When commission is collected:**
- Automatically deducted from your wallet when you mark an order "Completed"
- Commission is calculated on the **total paid amount** (after discounts, before delivery)
- For payment failures: no commission charged on failed orders
- If your wallet balance is insufficient at completion time, the commission is recorded as owed and collected from future balance

---

## 7. Payments & Payouts

> ⚠️ **Status disclosure (2026-08):** automatic crediting of net proceeds to seller wallets is
> under development. Until it ships, funds for completed orders are held by the platform and
> settlement is initiated by Nodbusiness after order completion — no fixed payout SLA is
> offered during the launch period.

**How you receive payment:**
1. Buyer pays via Chapa, Stripe, or COD
2. Order is marked "Completed" and verified
3. Net proceeds (order total minus 3% commission) are settled to your Nodbusiness wallet by Nodbusiness
4. You withdraw to your bank account via "Wallet → Withdraw" — requests are reviewed and processed within 1–3 business days

**Platform may hold payment when:**
- A buyer has opened a dispute
- Fraud investigation is in progress
- Your account has unresolved violations

---

## 8. Returns & Refunds

**Your obligations as a seller:**
- You must accept returns for items that are materially different from their listing
- You must accept returns for damaged items
- You are encouraged (not required) to accept change-of-mind returns

**Platform's role:**
- When a buyer opens a return request, you have 3 days to respond
- If no response, admin may automatically approve refund
- Approved refunds are deducted from your wallet balance

**Your protection:**
- You may reject unreasonable return requests with explanation
- Buyers who abuse the return system will be flagged
- Platform provides photo evidence in disputes

---

## 9. Prohibited Conduct

You may not:
- List the same product multiple times to inflate search results
- Use fake reviews or pay for reviews
- Accept payment outside the platform to avoid commission (platform ban)
- Share buyer personal data with third parties
- Post competitor links or redirect buyers to other platforms
- Create multiple seller accounts

---

## 10. Intellectual Property

- You must own or have rights to all images, text, and trademarks in your listings
- You grant Nodbusiness a license to display your content on the platform and in marketing
- Nodbusiness may use your seller name and product images in "Featured Seller" promotions
- You may opt out of promotional use by emailing sellers@nodbusiness.app

---

## 11. Tax Obligations

You are responsible for:
- Declaring income from Nodbusiness sales to the Ethiopian Revenue and Customs Authority (ERCA)
- Collecting and remitting VAT if your annual sales exceed the VAT registration threshold
- Nodbusiness will provide transaction records on request for tax reporting

Nodbusiness does NOT collect or remit taxes on your behalf (except where legally required).

---

## 12. Account Suspension & Termination

Nodbusiness may suspend or terminate your seller account for:
- Selling prohibited items
- Repeated order fulfillment failures
- Fraud or deception
- Harassment of buyers
- Violation of this agreement

**Appeal process:** You may appeal any suspension by emailing sellers@nodbusiness.app within 14 days.

---

## 13. Dispute Resolution

In case of disagreement with a buyer:
1. First attempt resolution through in-platform chat
2. If unresolved after 3 days, escalate to Nodbusiness mediation
3. Nodbusiness's decision in mediation is final and binding

For disputes with Nodbusiness itself: see Terms of Service §9.

---

## 14. Changes to This Agreement

Nodbusiness may update this agreement with 30 days' notice via in-app notification. Continued selling after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
