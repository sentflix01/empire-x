## Tier 1: Critical Partners (Must Have Before Launch)

### 1. Ethio Telecom / Telebirr

**Why critical:** Telebirr has 30M+ users — the largest payment network in Ethiopia. Chapa already supports Telebirr as a payment method, but a direct partnership unlocks:

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Joint promotion | Nodbusiness bundled with Telebirr app store |
| Lower fees | Reduced transaction fees (currently 1.5–2%) |
| Trust transfer | Ethio Telecom's brand trust → Nodbusiness |
| USSD access | Basic Nodbusiness functionality via USSD for feature phones |
| Data zero-rating | Nodbusiness doesn't count against Telebirr data bundles |

**Approach:** B2B pitch to Ethio Telecom Business Division. Offer Telebirr as exclusive primary payment method for 12 months.

---

### 2. Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE)

**Why important:** CBE has 40+ million accounts and is the most trusted financial institution in Ethiopia.

| Benefit | Details |

## Partnership Pitch Framework

When approaching any potential partner, lead with:

1. **Their customer problem:** "Your SMB customers need an online presence but don't know how to set one up"
2. **Nodbusiness solution:** "Nodbusiness gives them a storefront in 30 minutes"
3. **Joint benefit:** "You get to offer your customers more value; we get distribution"
4. **Proof:** "We have X sellers already generating ETB Y in GMV"
5. **Ask:** "Can we pilot with your top 100 SMB customers for 90 days?"

## Partnership Management

| Phase | Action |
|---|---|
| Outreach | LinkedIn + warm introduction (always better) |
| First meeting | 30 min demo + listening session |
| Pilot | 90-day limited partnership, measure outcomes |
| Full partnership | MOU, co-marketing agreement, revenue share if applicable |
| Renewal | Annual review, expand scope |

Track all partnerships in a CRM (HubSpot free tier or Airtable).
